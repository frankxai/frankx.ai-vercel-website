# MVU source-led recovery — scene brief

## Purpose

Turn `/mvu` from a generic event/service landing page into a living field atlas
that preserves Frank's actual Tallinn experience. The page must make a visible
distinction between Frank's words, editorial synthesis, and sourced practice
guides.

## Audience and task

- Frank: verify that the published record still sounds and feels like him.
- People met in Tallinn: find the relevant reflection, session, or practice.
- People outside the room: learn without being told that an editorial
  reconstruction is a verbatim journal.

Primary task: start with the first-person Week One reflection.
Secondary task: browse session intelligence and applied guides.

## Composition

1. Editorial hero with a real line from Frank's 28 July dictated reflection.
2. Three-layer provenance key: Frank's notes, field intelligence, practice guides.
3. Week One reflection as the dominant story.
4. Chronological event spine for the published session material.
5. Practice guide shelf, beginning with “Unhooking the Mind.”
6. Small official-event and independence statement.

The speculative lab and generic service cards are removed from the primary
story. The essay “Your Mind Is a Temporary Library” remains as a supporting
thesis, not the visual identity of the event record.

## Visual world

- Night editorial journal rather than startup landing page.
- Ink black and warm paper/amber accents, with the existing FrankX cyan used
  sparingly for navigation and source-state signals.
- Large typographic moments, hairline rules, restrained cards, no decorative
  generated media.
- The hero's visual object is a timestamped note card; the evidence itself is
  the image.

## Motion

CSS transitions only: link shifts, border/color changes, and subtle hover lift.
No scroll choreography. All transitions have `motion-reduce` fallbacks.

## Responsive and accessibility

- One-column reading order on mobile.
- Minimum 44px primary controls.
- Visible focus rings.
- Semantic sections, headings, lists, time elements, and article links.
- No color-only provenance distinction; every layer has a text label.

## Editorial boundary

- First-person note: light grammar, repetition, and privacy edit only.
- Field intelligence: clearly labeled synthesis; never presented as a transcript.
- Practice guides: source list and safety boundary required.
- Private relationship material and Arcanea mythology stay outside FrankX.
- Ambitions remain framed as ambitions, not current financial claims.
