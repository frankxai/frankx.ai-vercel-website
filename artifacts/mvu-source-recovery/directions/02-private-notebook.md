# Direction 02 — The Private Notebook

Status: explored, not selected

## Experience thesis

The recipient enters a quiet, intimate record of one week in Tallinn. The job is emotional proximity: hear Frank’s voice before encountering any model or framework.

The signature proof is one dated page at a time, with exact lines, crossed-out uncertainties, and marginal source notes.

The intended feeling is: **reading a trusted friend’s notebook after being invited—not browsing a knowledge product.**

## Visual and editorial system

- Warm paper, graphite, midnight ink, and a single red editorial mark.
- One entry per screen with minimal navigation.
- First-person writing dominates; synthesis moves into footnotes or a second reading mode.
- Session names remain secondary to time, place, and immediate observation.
- Audio snippets could eventually accompany the exact transcript where consent and provenance are clear.

## Primary action

Open today’s note.

## Strength

Highest intimacy and strongest distinction from the blog.

## Why it was not selected

It makes the field intelligence harder to discover, weakens comparison across sessions, and creates a visual metaphor that can become decorative or falsely “handwritten.” It also does less to solve the existing provenance problem across a growing archive.
