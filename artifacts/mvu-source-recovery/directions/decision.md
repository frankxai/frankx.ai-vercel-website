# MVU Source Recovery — Direction Decision

Selected: **Direction 01, The Provenance-Led Field Atlas**

## Decision

The atlas best resolves the actual product problem: the existing page looked polished but did not carry enough of Frank’s lived week, and its layers were not consistently traceable.

It preserves the strongest current UI, gives the new 25–29 July content an obvious home, and keeps first-person journal notes distinct from editorial synthesis and applied practices.

## Release contract

- Recipient: founder, creator, or participant seeking a trustworthy, useful record.
- Job: locate a real note and understand how it became an idea or practice.
- Primary action: read the latest reflection and browse the chronology.
- Signature proof: the 04:16 Week One note plus visible source boundaries.
- Intended feeling: a real notebook engineered into an atlas.
- Accessibility: semantic chronology, visible focus, readable line lengths, no interaction required to reach content.
- Responsive behaviour: single-column reading first; cards and metadata enhance at larger widths.
- Performance: content-led layout, no graph runtime or decorative media dependency.
- Content truth: exact personal quotes remain exact; paraphrase, interpretation, and exclusion are labelled.

## Deferred directions

The Private Notebook may inform future journal art direction. The Session Constellation becomes appropriate only after the archive contains enough stable, attributed nodes to justify an accessible graph.
