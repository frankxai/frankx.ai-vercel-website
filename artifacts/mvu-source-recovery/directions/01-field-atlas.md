# Direction 01 — The Provenance-Led Field Atlas

Status: selected

## Experience thesis

The recipient is a founder, creator, or fellow MVU participant who wants to understand what Frank actually experienced without mistaking synthesis for transcript.

The page helps them find one real note, follow it into one usable idea, and see exactly where the source changes from speaker input to Frank’s interpretation.

The signature proof is the 04:16 Week One reflection beside a day-by-day, source-labelled chronology.

The intended feeling is: **inside a real notebook that has been engineered into an atlas—not sold a generic event service.**

## Visual and editorial system

- Near-black editorial canvas with warm amber for Frank’s words, technical cyan for synthesis, and restrained green for applied practices.
- Chronology is the primary spine.
- Three explicit layers: Frank’s note, field intelligence, practice guide.
- Dates and session labels outrank invented “Day N” continuity.
- Quotes appear only when they are Frank’s exact recorded words or independently verified.
- Compact source-boundary panels stay adjacent to the content they qualify.

## Primary action

Read the latest first-person reflection, then browse the field atlas.

## Why it can scale

Markdown files become entries without a page-code change. The layer system survives new notes, later attribution corrections, and the addition of practice tools without collapsing the collections into one voice.
