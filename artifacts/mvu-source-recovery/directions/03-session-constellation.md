# Direction 03 — The Session Constellation

Status: explored, not selected

## Experience thesis

The recipient sees the week as a network rather than a diary. Sessions, ideas, questions, products, and experiments become nodes connected by shared themes such as identity, receiving, regulation, evidence, and distribution.

The signature proof is the visible path from one live note through Frank’s interpretation to a concrete experiment.

The intended feeling is: **watching a week of experience become a living knowledge graph.**

## Visual and editorial system

- Deep spatial canvas with restrained points, lines, and semantic colour.
- Theme nodes open day notes and source cards.
- Relationships are explicit: speaker input → direct observation → Frank synthesis → applied experiment.
- A linear reading mode remains available for keyboard and small-screen use.

## Primary action

Choose a theme and follow its evidence trail.

## Strength

Best expression of “massive knowledgebase,” and strongest at showing cross-day relationships.

## Why it was not selected

The graph adds interaction cost before enough canonical content exists. On mobile and assistive technology it requires a substantial parallel experience. It could make a simple editorial truth feel like a software demo. The current release needs content density and trust before spatial exploration.
