# Frame — support triage

## fixture notice

This is a worked example distributed with the AI Architect plugin. The company, the
repository, the file paths, the volumes and the command outputs below are a **fixture**:
they are stated here as the example's definition, not recorded from a live system. The
artifact set is internally checkable — every number in `04-roi.md` re-derives from
`prices.json` and the assumptions in this file — and it is not evidence about any real
company. Anything you copy from here has to be re-derived against your own repository
before it counts as evidence.

## outcome

Every inbound support email is classified, routed to a queue, and answered with a drafted
reply that a support agent approves before anything leaves the building.

## non-goals

- Sending a reply without a human clicking send. The draft is the product; the send is not.
- Answering billing or contract questions. Those route to the queue a person owns and stop there.
- Replacing the support platform. The system reads and writes through it and owns no ticket state.
- Learning from corrections automatically. Corrections become eval cases a person promotes, not
  weights or prompt edits that change behaviour between runs.
- Handling live chat. This is the email inbox only.

## kill criterion

If, after four full weeks in production, the share of drafted replies that support agents send
without editing the body is below one in three, measured on 2026-10-04 from the support
platform's revision history, the drafting step is removed and only classification and routing
stay. Measurement command and its owner are in `07-runbook.md`, step 6.

The classification half has its own floor: if routing accuracy against the support lead's weekly
sample of 50 tickets is below 90 out of 100 correct on the same date, the whole system is turned
off, not tuned.
