Today I caught myself spending too much of the morning and afternoon indoors, moving between laptops, ideas, and everything I'm building.

And I was reminded of something simple.

**We're building an extraordinary future with AI. But we still live in human bodies.**

We need daylight. Fresh air. Movement. Nature. Moments where our minds aren't processing another screen, another task, another possibility.

Especially in autumn. Especially when it's raining. Especially when work feels too important to interrupt.

I've come to believe that taking care of our physical health isn't separate from building something meaningful. It's part of what makes the work sustainable.

A walk outside might not look productive on a dashboard. But sometimes it's exactly what the person behind that dashboard needs.

**Build the future. Don't forget to live in it.**
