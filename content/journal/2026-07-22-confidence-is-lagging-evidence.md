Today connected intuition and courage in a way I can use.

Intuition can propose a move. It cannot prove the move.

Courage makes it testable.

Reality returns evidence.

Discernment decides whether to continue, close the door, or pivot.

I keep letting one act of exposure become a final referendum. One page decides whether I am a writer. One invitation decides whether I belong. One offer decides whether I have authority.

That is not the real game.

The real game is staying present long enough to learn, recover, build skill, and let asymmetric upside find me.

> “Confidence predicts from the past. Courage creates new evidence.”

My action is not to manufacture confidence. It is to choose one public move with a bounded downside, record what I fear, make the move, and watch both the external result and my recovery.
