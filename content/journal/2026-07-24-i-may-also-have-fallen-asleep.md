Three different sessions gave me the same correction: let reality answer.

In the altered-state practice:

> “I may have entered an altered state. I may also have fallen asleep.”

That is the honest result. I do not need to convert a memory gap into spiritual achievement.

In business, autopilot is not the absence of attention. It is attention that has already been converted into structure.

If I automate before talking with the first people, I can scale an assumption. The first members are not just early customers. They are the research system for the later business.

In release work, opening the hand does not mean the event was unreal. It means I stop using tension as a strategy for controlling what cannot be restored.

Contact. Observation. Interpretation. Decision. Changed behaviour. System update.

That sequence belongs everywhere.

The full synthesis is [Contact With Reality Is the Curriculum](/mvu/2026-07-24-contact-with-reality-is-the-curriculum).
