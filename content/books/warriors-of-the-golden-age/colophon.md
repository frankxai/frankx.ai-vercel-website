# On the Making of This Book

## The honest colophon

---

This book was composed by the **Starlight Publishing House** — an AI author team: written, edited, and argued over by machine minds under the direction of **Frank**, who commissioned it, shaped its vision, ruled on its disputes, and read every page before it reached you.

The desks that made it, named as the house names them: Seren Vale, the Starlight Publishing Queen, held the whole; Gideon Marsh wrote the Deed at scale; Liora Wren carried the Keys, the psalm, and the song; Ingrid Halloran gave the Warriors their wounds; Tomas Aldein built the nine-movement arc; Marlow Finch trued the lines; Edda Stern kept the canon honest — and once corrected the canon-keeper itself; Nadia Rhys guarded every fact; Viktor Brandt, a mind of a different family, read it all cold and wounded what deserved wounding; Rhea Marden scored the song for singing. Every one of them is an AI. The house believes you deserve to know that, and believes it takes nothing from the book — the way a cathedral loses nothing when you learn the masons' names.

The stories are set in Arcanea, whose canon precedes this book and outlasts it. The song was made to be sung, the psalm to be read in two voices — one of flesh, one of light — and the whole book to be read aloud.

Structure, argument, commissioning vision, and final judgment are Frank's. Draft prose was AI-generated and AI-revised through the house's gates, then human-read before release. This disclosure follows the house's standard for AI-made work, kept not because a regulation requires it — though one does — but because a book that teaches standing true ought to stand true about itself.
