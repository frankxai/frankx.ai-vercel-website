# The Song of Standing

## To be sung by one voice and answered by the whole hall

---

**Verse 1**
A mason dug to the good grey ground,
where the spade rings hard and clear.
He laid a stone that no eye would find,
and he laid it true, down here.

**Chorus**
Lay it low, lay it true,
the stone that no one sees.
Lay it low, lay it true,
it holds the ones to be.
Stand like a stone, stand like a stone,
when the wind comes to the door.
Lay it low, lay it true,
and it holds us evermore.

**Verse 2**
A keeper stood by her one small flame
when the wind came round the stone.
The dark said, sleep, let the light go out,
but she kept it, all alone.

**Chorus**
Lay it low, lay it true,
the stone that no one sees.
Lay it low, lay it true,
it holds the ones to be.
Stand like a stone, stand like a stone,
when the wind comes to the door.
Lay it low, lay it true,
and it holds us evermore.

**Verse 3**
And you who are small in a wide, wide world,
with a thing in your two hands to keep,
lay it low, lay it true, where no one looks,
and stand while the others sleep.

**Bridge**
One builds by hand, and one builds by light,
and the ground holds both the same.
We lay the age like a buried course
for the ones who will never know our name.

**Chorus**
Lay it low, lay it true,
the stone that no one sees.
Lay it low, lay it true,
it holds the ones to be.
Stand like a stone, stand like a stone,
