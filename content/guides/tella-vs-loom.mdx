Pick Loom when the video is a quick message to your own team, someone records from a phone, or you need a free plan. Pick Tella when the video goes to customers, investors or students and you will reuse it. That is the whole decision for most people. The rest of this page is the evidence, with every price checked against both vendors' pricing pages on 20 October 2026, on both annual and monthly billing, because the answer on price flips with the billing period.

Disclosure: links to Tella on this page are referral links. If you subscribe, I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change the comparison.

## The verdict by job

| The job | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A 30-second reply to a colleague | Loom | Fastest from record to link; free plan covers it |
| Recording from your phone | Loom | Tella has no iOS or Android app |
| Homepage or launch demo | Tella | Zooms, layouts, backgrounds and 4K export |
| Investor or board update | Either | Both offer viewer insights; pick Tella if you want to edit the update before sending |
| Sales follow-up from a laptop | Tella | Branded share page with a CTA button |
| Sales follow-up from the field | Loom | Mobile apps; recipients know the link format |
| Course lesson or YouTube tutorial | Tella | Clip-based recording, transcript editing, chapters |
| Meeting notes and recaps | Loom | Auto-meeting notes are part of Business + AI |
| Team in Jira and Confluence | Loom | Atlassian owns Loom and integrates it |
| Agent workflows (MCP, Claude, ChatGPT) | Tella | MCP server, Claude connector, ChatGPT app, public API |

## Prices, on both billing periods

Checked on [tella.com/pricing](https://www.tella.com/pricing) and [loom.com/pricing](https://www.loom.com/pricing) on 20 October 2026, per user.

| Plan | Annual billing, per month | Annual billing, per year | Monthly billing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loom Starter | Free | Free | Free |
| Loom Business | $15 | $180 | $18 |
| Loom Business + AI | $20 | $240 | $24 |
| Tella Pro | $13 | $156 | $26 |
| Tella Premium | $19 | $228 | $39 |

Three things decide the price question:

1. **Transcript editing and filler-word removal.** Tella includes them in Pro. Loom puts them in Business + AI. If you want them, compare Tella Pro ($156 a year) with Loom Business + AI ($240 a year).
2. **Billing period.** On monthly billing Tella costs more than either Loom tier. Annual billing is where Tella is cheaper.
3. **The free plan.** Loom Starter is free with 25 videos and 5-minute screen recordings. Tella has a 7-day trial and nothing free after it.

For a team of five on annual billing: Tella Pro is $780 a year, Loom Business $900, Loom Business + AI $1,200. The full Tella breakdown is in [Tella pricing](/guides/tella-pricing).

## Where Loom wins

Loom is the default for good reasons. Recording to a shareable link takes seconds. The mobile apps mean a support lead can answer a ticket from a phone. The free plan covers occasional use. Inside an Atlassian company, Loom links sit next to Jira tickets and Confluence pages. And its AI tier has moved into meetings: notes, recaps and recordings that agents can read.

If most of your videos are internal and disposable, Loom is the cheaper and faster choice.

## Where Tella wins

Tella is built for the video somebody outside your company will judge. You record in clips and edit by deleting words. Automatic zooms follow your clicks, layouts switch between camera and screen, and Studio Voice cleans the audio. The share page carries your branding and a CTA button, and analytics show where viewers drop off. Premium moves share links onto your own domain.

It also works on Windows with automatic zooms, which matters because the best-known polish tool, Screen Studio, is Mac only.

## Switching from Loom

If you move a team, plan it in three steps:

1. **Run both for two weeks.** Keep Loom for internal replies. Record every external video in Tella.
2. **Move the library you still use.** Tella ships a Loom migration tool and a [switching guide](https://www.tella.com/switch). Only move videos people still watch.
3. **Check the seat math.** Tella advertises a [buyout offer](https://www.tella.com/loom-buyout) for teams of 20 or more seats. Read the terms before you cancel Loom.

Running both is a legitimate end state: Loom for quick internal messages, Tella for the videos you edit before sending.

## How to decide in one week

Do not choose from a feature table. Run this test:

| Day | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Write one 90-second demo with the [script template](/downloads/demo-video-script-template.md) |
| 2 | Record it in Loom and in Tella, same script, same screen |
| 3 | Edit both to the best result you can get in 15 minutes |
| 4 | Show both versions to the same three people who would actually watch it |
| 5 to 7 | Compare their feedback, your editing time and the yearly cost, then pick |

Six opinions will not settle which tool is better in general. They will tell you which one fits your videos and your audience. Tella's trial exports HD at 30 FPS, so compare both tools at 1080p rather than judging Tella's paid 4K output.
