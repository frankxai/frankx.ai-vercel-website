Start with thirteen seconds of wire. Pause before it changes shape. Sketch the bend, play the rest, then sketch it again. You now have evidence of motion. To explain what moved *inside* the metal, you need another source.

## Watch one change twice

On the first viewing, write a sentence using only what the camera shows. On the second, name a cause you could test. A useful sentence about the indigo Short starts with what happens to one fold of cloth after it comes out of the vat. A useful chemical explanation follows the dye through its soluble reduced state and its return to the blue form.

- **Indigo.** Track colour at the same point on the fabric. Compare the sequence with a measurement of the redox reaction. [Electrochemical study of indigo](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0013-4686(02)00028-2)
- **Tin.** Track a branch tip on the growing deposit. Inspect the published micrographs to see how researchers characterise dendrites at a much smaller scale. [Tin dendrite morphology](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/12/7/1201)
- **Nitinol.** Write down which shape the wire recovers. Use the phase diagram to identify what else you would need to know about temperature, load and history. [Sandia’s shape-memory model](https://www.sandia.gov/files/sierra/SM_LAME_5_30/main/models/shape_memory_alloy.html)
- **Bismuth.** Identify the moment the sample begins to flow. A reference melting point gives you a number against which a thermometer could be checked. [Royal Society of Chemistry element record](https://periodic-table.rsc.org/element/83/bismuth)

## Read a museum image

In the British Museum film, look for the point at which a different way of seeing changes the question the conservator can ask. Next, open Figure 5 of Verri and colleagues’ 2023 paper. Compare the visible image of the mantle of East Pediment figure L with the luminescence image. Place a finger over one surviving patch. How much of the surrounding motif could you defend from that patch alone?

Draw the surviving marks in one colour and your inferred border in another. Erosion and a dark crust complicate the map. An area without a signal leaves more than one explanation open. [Figure 5 and the textiles discussion](https://doi.org/10.15184/aqy.2023.130)

## Audit an AI materials claim

Give the design target, the proposed crystal, the realised sample and the measured property four separate lines. In the MatterGen example, the target was a bulk modulus of 200 GPa. The authors measured 169 GPa. Their notes about disorder in the sample matter just as much as the numerical gap. One reported sample leaves the success rate across all candidates open. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08628-5) · [Researchers’ account](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/mattergen-a-new-paradigm-of-materials-design-with-generative-ai/)

## Change the rules in fiction

Imagine a ceramic cup that stores the potter’s final brushstroke. A ferryman can read the route inside it once. The act of reading erases the mark. Who should open the cup before the crossing, and what would persuade anyone else to trust their account?

The one-use memory is invented. The creative borrowing is the idea of a hidden signal in a material. Give the next writer both pieces of information. For another approach to the cost of transmutation in fiction, read the [official introduction to Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood](https://fullmetalalchemistusa.com/introduction/).
