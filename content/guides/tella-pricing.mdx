You are here because the Tella prices you found elsewhere do not agree with each other. Review sites quote anything from $12 to $49, and several still say Tella has a free plan. I checked the [official pricing page](https://www.tella.com/pricing) on 20 October 2026, including the monthly-billing toggle, so this page gives you the current numbers, what each plan is for and what a year of Tella actually costs your team.

The short version: Pro is $13 per user per month billed annually, or $26 billed monthly. Premium is $19 or $39. There is no free plan. The cheapest honest test is the 7-day trial with one real video.

## Tella plans and prices

Checked on [tella.com/pricing](https://www.tella.com/pricing) on 20 October 2026.

| | Pro | Premium | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Billed annually | $13 per user per month | $19 per user per month | Custom |
| Billed monthly | $26 per user per month | $39 per user per month | Custom |
| Agent credits | 3,000 per user per month | 6,000 per user per month | Per contract |
| Videos | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Export | Up to 4K | Up to 4K, plus 60 FPS | Up to 4K, plus 60 FPS |
| Captions | 106 languages | 106 languages | 106 languages |
| Analytics history | 30 days | All time, advanced | All time, advanced |
| Custom domain for share links | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove Tella branding | No | Yes | Yes |
| SSO and SCIM | Add-on | Add-on | Included |
| SOC 2 Type II report | No | No | Yes |

Annual billing cuts the per-month price by about half. Tella labels the annual toggle "50% off".

## There is no free plan, only a trial

Tella's [help centre](https://www.tella.com/help/billing/how-the-free-trial-works) is direct about it: there is no forever-free plan. What you get instead:

- **7 days of Pro** for every new workspace, no credit card.
- **Trial limits:** clips up to 2 hours and HD exports at 30 FPS.
- **One extension** of the trial if you need more time.
- **No Agent credits** during the trial.
- **After the trial:** recording, editing and exporting lock. Videos you made stay viewable.

Plan the trial so it produces something you would pay for. Day 1: set up branding and record a 90-second demo using the [script template](/downloads/demo-video-script-template.md). Day 2 to 5: send it to real people and watch the analytics. Day 6: decide. If nobody watched past ten seconds, fix the opening before you blame the tool.

## What credits are, and why most people can ignore them

Credits pay for one thing: [Tella Agent](https://www.tella.com/docs/changelog), the chat-based editor that entered public beta on 16 October 2026. You describe an edit, and the Agent makes it.

Recording, transcript editing, Auto Cut, Auto Zoom, layouts, captions and exports do not use credits. If you edit by hand, credits never matter.

| Credit fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Monthly allowance | 3,000 per user on Pro, 6,000 on Premium |
| Pooling | Shared across the workspace |
| Expiry | Unused monthly credits expire at the end of the month |
| Top-ups | $10 for 10,000, $45 for 50,000, $80 for 100,000, bought by the workspace owner |
| Top-up expiry | Purchased credits never expire |

Run a few Agent edits on the monthly allowance before you buy a top-up. The feature is in beta, and you want to know how many credits a typical edit uses on your own videos.

## Pro or Premium?

Pick by what leaves the building.

| You need | Plan |
|---|---|
| Internal updates, tutorials, course lessons, YouTube | Pro |
| Share links on your own domain, for example video.yourcompany.com | Premium |
| No Tella branding on the share page | Premium |
| 60 FPS exports for smooth scrolling and cursor motion | Premium |
| Analytics older than 30 days, for example quarterly reporting | Premium |
| SSO, SCIM, SOC 2 report for procurement | Enterprise |

Most solo founders and creators should start on Pro. Upgrade when a customer-facing page starts carrying your videos and the Tella branding or the 30-day analytics window gets in the way.

Monthly billing makes sense for a short project or a trial month after the free week. If you expect to record for more than six months, annual costs about half as much.

### Cost per video

Because videos are unlimited, the cost per video falls as you record more. On Pro billed annually ($13 a month):

| Videos a month | Cost per video |
|---|---|
| 2 | $6.50 |
| 4 | $3.25 |
| 10 | $1.30 |
| 20 | $0.65 |

Compare that with the hour you spend in a call that a 3-minute video could replace. For most teams, the subscription is not the expensive part. Recording time is.

## How to pay less

- **Use the trial properly.** Seven days is enough to record, send and measure one real video.
- **Choose annual billing** once you know you will keep recording. It costs about half the monthly rate.
- **Start on Pro.** Upgrade to Premium when your domain or branding needs it.
- **Check education and nonprofit programs.** Tella runs [Tella for Education](https://www.tella.com/for-education) and a nonprofit program; the terms are on those pages; the pricing page does not list them.
- **Referral discount.** [Tella's affiliate page](https://www.tella.com/affiliate-program) says new paying users who arrive through a partner link get 20% off their first three months; I could not see the discount on the landing page, so check it at checkout.
