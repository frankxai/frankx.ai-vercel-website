The usual reasons to leave Tella are clear: there is no free plan, no phone app, and on a Mac some people prefer the specific look Screen Studio produces. This page sorts the alternatives by the reason you are leaving, so you can skip to yours and end with a next step in the tool you pick. Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page as recorded on 20 October 2026, with the billing period named, because annual and monthly prices often differ by half.

This page contains no referral links. Links go to each tool's official site.

## First decide what kind of tool you need

Many "alternatives" lists mix tools that do different jobs. Put yours in the right row first.

| Category | What the viewer does | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| Polished screen recording | Watches a narrated, edited video | Tella, Screen Studio, FocuSee, Camtasia, Cap Studio Mode, ScreenPal, CleanShot X |
| Async video messaging | Watches a quick unedited clip | Loom, Cap Instant Mode, Zight, Bubbles |
| Text-based editing | Watches a long edited recording | Descript |
| Interactive demos | Clicks through the product themselves | Arcade, Supademo, Storylane, Navattic |
| Written guides | Reads steps with screenshots | Scribe, Guidde (video variant) |
| AI avatar video | Watches a generated presenter | Synthesia, HeyGen |
| Remote studio | Watches an interview or podcast | Riverside |

Tella sits in the first row with the hosting layer of the second. Its real substitutes are the tools that do both, or the pair of tools you would otherwise combine.

## Screen Studio: the Mac polish benchmark

Screen Studio is a macOS app known for automatic zooms on clicks, smoothed cursor movement and camera layouts that make a plain screen recording look produced. It exports 4K at 60 FPS, records iPhone and iPad screens over USB with device frames, and includes transcript editing with filler and pause removal.

- **Price:** $9 a month billed annually or $29 monthly. The one-time licence is no longer sold to new buyers.
- **Platforms:** Mac only. Its FAQ says there are no near-future plans for Windows.
- **Limits:** share links run up to 30 minutes, and it is built for individuals rather than team workspaces.

**Choose Screen Studio** if you are a Mac user making launch videos, YouTube tutorials or social clips and you publish the files elsewhere. **Stay on Tella** if anyone on your team uses Windows, or you want hosted share pages, CTAs and analytics.

## Loom: the async default

Loom is the fastest way to send a quick screen and camera message. It runs on Mac, Windows, iOS, Android and Chrome, and Atlassian has owned it since 2023.

- **Price:** Starter free (25 videos, 5-minute screen recordings). Business $15 per user per month billed annually or $18 monthly. Business + AI, which adds filler-word removal and transcript editing, $20 billed annually or $24 monthly.
- **Strengths:** mobile apps, a free plan, Jira and Confluence integration, meeting notes and recaps in the AI tier.
- **Limits:** a basic editor below the AI tier; per-seat pricing adds up.

The full head-to-head is in [Tella vs Loom](/guides/tella-vs-loom).

## Descript: when editing is the job

Descript is an editor that happens to record. You edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with tools for filler-word removal, studio sound, translation and dubbing.

- **Price:** Free plan with 1 media hour a month, 720p and a watermark. Hobbyist $16 per person per month billed annually or $24 monthly, with 10 media hours. Creator $24 billed annually or $35 monthly, with 30 hours.
- **Strengths:** long recordings, podcasts, interviews, multi-track audio.
- **Limits:** media hours and AI credits are metered per plan, so heavy recorders hit the cap before they hit a video count.

**Tella vs Descript, in one line:** record screen and camera videos in Tella; edit podcasts and long-form recordings in Descript. Some creators use both.

## Cap: open source, cross-platform

Cap is an open-source recorder for Mac, Windows and Linux with two modes: Instant Mode for a share link right away and Studio Mode with a local editor.

- **Price (in euros):** free for personal use with 4K/60 export and 5-minute share links. Desktop licence €29 a year for commercial local use. Pro €12 per user per month, or €8.16 billed annually, adds unlimited cloud sharing, a custom domain, password-protected shares, viewer analytics, AI titles and chapters, team workspaces, a Loom importer and a custom S3 bucket.
- **Strengths:** open source, self-hosting, data on your own storage, a low price.
- **Limits:** a smaller feature set than Tella; filler-word removal is not listed on its pricing page.

Cap is the pick for developers, privacy-sensitive teams and anyone who wants their recordings in their own bucket.

## Camtasia: the full editor for training teams

Camtasia from TechSmith is a traditional multitrack editor with a recorder, cursor effects, quizzes and SCORM export for learning management systems. It runs on Mac and Windows, with a browser version.

- **Price:** subscription only since the 2025 release, billed yearly in tiers. The entry tier exports with a watermark. Check [TechSmith's store](https://www.techsmith.com/store/camtasia) for your currency.
- **Choose it** when an LMS requires SCORM, or when you need a full timeline with many layers.
- **Skip it** for quick demos and updates; it is slower than record-and-share tools.

## On Windows: what to use instead of Screen Studio

There is no Screen Studio for Windows. These run natively on Windows and add automatic zooms from your clicks:

| Tool | Auto zoom | Hosting and share links | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tella | Yes | Yes, with analytics | From $13 per user per month billed annually ($26 monthly) |
| Cap | Yes | Yes; unlimited on Pro | Free for personal use; Pro €8.16 billed annually |
| FocuSee | Yes, following clicks and typing | Share links | Not listed on the vendor's page |
| Camtasia | Smart Focus | Through TechSmith's sharing | Subscription, billed yearly |
| OBS Studio | No | No | Free |

Two open-source projects, OpenScreen and its fork Recordly, have appeared as free Screen Studio-style recorders. Reviews describe them as early, and the maintainer calls OpenScreen beta. Try them on a side project before trusting a client deliverable to them.

## When the answer is not a recorder

Three jobs look like screen recording but are better served by something else:

- **The prospect should try the product.** Interactive demo tools capture your product as a click-through experience. Arcade, Supademo, Storylane and Navattic all have free entry points, and the [product demo video guide](/guides/product-demo-video-guide) explains when to use video, an interactive demo, or both.
- **The reader needs steps to follow.** Scribe turns a workflow into a written guide with screenshots. Guidde makes narrated how-to videos from browser captures.
- **Nobody should be on camera.** Synthesia and HeyGen generate a presenter from a script. They generate the presenter and leave your product screen out; for founder and executive messages a real recording is more credible. [Async video for founders](/guides/async-video-for-founders) covers that choice.
