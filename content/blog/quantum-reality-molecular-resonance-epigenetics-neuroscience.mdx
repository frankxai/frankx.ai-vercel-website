## 1. Bioelectricity and Morphogenetic Fields: Beyond the Genome

- **Non-Neural Bioelectric Networks**: All somatic cells utilize ion channels (sodium, potassium, calcium, chloride) to maintain resting membrane potentials.

## 2. Molecular Epigenetics: How Mental States Alter Gene Expression

Epigenetics is the study of changes in gene expression that do not alter the underlying nucleotide sequence. Environmental stimuli, emotional states, and sustained mental focus modulate gene expression through precise biochemical pathways:

1. **Histone Acetylation & Methylation**: Tight chromatin packaging prevents transcription factors from binding to DNA. Sustained stress or chronic sympathetic arousal upregulates pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha) via NF-kB pathways.
2. **Upregulation of Neurotrophic Factors**: Focused contemplative states, deep flow, and coherent autonomic regulation stimulate the synthesis of **Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF)** and neuroplastic gene cassettes.
3. **MicroRNA Regulation**: Intracellular neuropeptide release triggers microRNA transcription that selectively silences inflammatory pathways while upregulating antioxidant and cellular repair proteins.
