### Level any free or $5 AI voiceover with one ffmpeg command

Raw TTS exports often arrive too quiet, sibilant, or uneven against background music. Run every voiceover file through this single `ffmpeg` loudness and high-pass chain before dropping it onto your CapCut timeline:

```bash
# High-pass rumble below 80Hz, gentle presence lift, and loudness normalization to -14 LUFS (a common streaming reference, not a published YouTube spec)
ffmpeg -i raw-voiceover.mp3 \
  -af "highpass=f=80,acompressor=threshold=-18dB:ratio=3:attack=15:release=180,loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=9" \
  -ar 48000 -b:a 192k voiceover-youtube-master.mp3
```

When mixing background Suno instrumentals underneath narration, duck the music bed to `-26 LUFS` (`12 dB` below voice) so spoken consonants stay crisp on phone speakers.

## What's the best tool to turn long videos into Shorts automatically?

This is the multiplier. One long upload becomes 5–10 vertical clips for Shorts, TikTok, and Reels — the same work, several times the reach.

**Opus Clip** is the category leader. **Starter is $15/month** for 150 processing minutes, no watermark, all caption styles, and 720p export. **Pro is $29/month** for 300 minutes, AI virality scoring, 1080p, and multi-platform auto-posting. Opus Clip's virality score genuinely predicts which clips land, and it's another tool creators keep paying for.

**AutoShorts.ai** plays a different game: it generates *entire* faceless videos end-to-end and auto-posts them. Starter is $19/month (post 3×/week), Daily is $39/month, Hardcore is $69/month. Good if you want full automation; weaker if you want control.

**[Faceless.so](https://faceless.so)** is an all-in-one faceless video generator — script through render, plus TTS, captions, and auto-posting across platforms — starting at $24/month. Same trade-off as AutoShorts: convenience over craft.

**Verdict:** For repurposing your own long videos, **Opus Clip Starter ($15)** is the buy. For zero-touch faceless generation, AutoShorts at $19 is the cheaper full-auto option — but don't run both.
