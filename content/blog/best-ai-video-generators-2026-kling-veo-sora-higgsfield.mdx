## Jobs that matter for FrankX / GenCreator

1. Architecture explainer (15–30s)  
2. Product hero loop  
3. Fitness/cinematic brand reel  
4. Faceless YouTube B-roll  
5. Social native short  

## Model roles (2026)

| Class | Strength | Typical use |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Kling-class | Stylized storytelling, motion | Brand reels, narrative shorts |
| Veo-class | Realism, camera language | Product demos, lifestyle |
| Sora-class | Complex scene generation | Concept films, story beats |
| Seedance-class | Commercial polish | Ads, launch films |
| Runway/Luma/Pika-class | Editing + iteration | Rapid social cuts |
| Higgsfield-class platforms | Multi-model access + control | Unified production desk |

## Production pipeline

1. Script beats (3–5 shots)  
2. Still keyframes from image stack  
3. Image-to-video or text-to-video per shot  
4. Audio (ElevenLabs / music OS)  
5. Edit (Descript / CapCut / Remotion)  
6. Brand QA (motion, text, claims)  

## Cost control rules

- Draft on cheaper/faster models  
- Final on premium  
- Cap seconds per post  
- Reuse motion templates  
- Never generate full videos before shot list  

## Platform vs single model

A multi-model desk (Higgsfield-class) wins when you:

- switch models per shot  
- need camera/motion control  
- run batch production for Studio  

Single-model loyalty dies when the job changes.

## AEO: What is the best AI video generator in 2026?

No single winner. Use Kling-class for stylized storytelling, Veo-class for realism, Sora-class for complex scenes, Seedance-class for commercial work, and a multi-model desk (e.g. Higgsfield-class) to route jobs without tool thrash.
