The shot that matters is the one you still need for the edit. A YouTube insert can survive a few cut frames. A product shot fails the moment the label changes, even when the camera move looks great. Choose two tools for that specific shot, give them the same inputs, and price what makes it onto the timeline.

*Updated November 24, 2026. Model capabilities and example rates come from provider documentation. The budget below uses illustrative results.*

## What happened to Sora?

OpenAI shut down the Sora web experience and app on April 26, 2026, and the API on September 24, 2026. The March version of this article recommended Sora for campaign shots and cited ChatGPT subscription prices. For a new campaign, shortlist a tool you can run today. For an existing pipeline, replace the Sora endpoint and test your old briefs against the replacement before sending it a batch. [OpenAI’s shutdown notice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation)

## Which AI video model fits the shot?

| Shot or production job | Where to start | What to check on your material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B-roll from an approved still | Runway Gen-4.5 | Does the camera move leave a clean cut point? |
| Choreography or a long camera move | Kling 4.0 or Seedance 2.5 | Does the subject hold its shape and position through the move? |
| Dialogue, ambience or an action timed to sound | Google Veo 3.1 or Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash | Does the sound land on the right frame, and can you replace it cleanly? |
| A revision to a shot you already have | Runway Aleph 2.0 or Luma Ray3.2 | Do untouched parts of the frame survive the edit? |
| A stylized transition or transformation | Pika | Can you cut into and out of the effect cleanly? |

### Runway Gen-4.5 and Aleph 2.0

Gen-4.5 takes a prompt or a starting image and supports clips of 2–10 seconds. Aleph 2.0 edits existing footage with keyframe guidance. When a take already has the performance and framing you want, start by asking for one localized change. Review the edges and adjacent frames for drift. [Gen-4.5 guide](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/46974685288467-Creating-with-Gen-4-5) · [Runway API models](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/models/)

### Kling 4.0 and Kling 4.0 Flash

Kuaishou’s Kling 4.0 supports up to 30 seconds per generation and accepts multiple references and keyframes. Runway Dev offers a 3–30 second route at 720p or 1080p, plus a 3–20 second Kling 4.0 Flash route at 720p. Start with the hardest stretch of the action, such as the point where a dancer’s hands pass in front of the face. Approve that movement before paying for a longer take. [Kuaishou release](https://ir.kuaishou.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kling-ai-officially-launches-next-generation-kling-40-raising/) · [Runway API release notes](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/changelog/)

### Google Veo 3.1 and Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash

Veo 3.1 generates video with native audio. Google Cloud documents 4-, 6- and 8-second clips for its Veo 3.1 model, with an eight-second requirement for reference-image generation. Try Veo 3.1 Fast or Lite on Google Cloud for blocking, then audition the final shot with the audio on. [Google Cloud model docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/veo/3-1-generate)

The separate Gemini Developer API retired its Veo 3.1 preview endpoints on October 22, 2026. For a Gemini API integration, evaluate Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash. It supports up to 15 seconds and conversational edits. [Gemini API deprecations](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/deprecations) · [Gemini Omni release](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/)

### Seedance 2.5

ByteDance documents up to 30 seconds of audio and video in one pass. Its image, video and audio references give you a way to specify what the shot should carry over. Test a single continuous move with a clear exit frame. A longer take only earns its cost when the shot still works near the end. [ByteDance release](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)

### Luma Ray3.2 and Pika

Luma Ray3.2 supports multiple keyframes and video-to-video editing. Use it when you have frames you need to hit or footage you want to revise. Pika’s effects are useful for a deliberately visible transformation. For a music video, start the effect a few frames before the beat and leave room after the transformation so you have a choice in the edit. [Luma Ray3.2 release](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2) · [Pika effects](https://pika.art/pikaffects)

## Budget for footage you can cut

Runway Dev lists Gen-4.5 at **$0.12 per generated second** and Kling 3.0 Standard at **$0.09 without audio or $0.13 with audio**. Those are USD developer rates on one platform. Consumer plans, taxes, audio, resolution and the input route can change the cost. A subscription’s starting price tells you little until you know which model your credits buy. [Runway API pricing](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/)

Here is an example using those rates and hypothetical results. Say you request ten eight-second takes of the same shot, with no generated audio.

| Runway Dev route | Generation spend | Seconds accepted after trimming | Spend per accepted second |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Gen-4.5 | $9.60 | 24 | $0.40 |
| Kling 3.0 Standard | $7.20 | 16 | $0.45 |

The lower rate still costs more per usable second in this example. Log the debits for every billed take, including rejects. Add paid upscales, edits and extensions to the generation bill. Track editor time separately so you can see when a cheap take costs an hour of cleanup.

Put three constraints on the first test. Pick one reference you have the right to use, specify one action and one camera move, and write down what would make you reject a take. For a product on a turntable, that could be an altered logo, wandering reflections or a warped cap. Compare at the same aspect ratio and duration where supported. Note any settings that differ and review the footage at delivery size. [FrankX shot-based evaluation method](/blog/best-ai-video-generators-2026-x-aggregated)

## Put each tool where it helps the edit

**Short-form social.** Test the motion in the delivery aspect ratio before you batch. On a 9:16 clip, make sure the subject survives the caption area and interface overlays.

**YouTube and product demos.** Generate atmosphere, inserts and transitions. Record the real interface when you need to show where a button lives or what a feature does. For a physical product, hold an approved pack shot, label and silhouette next to every generated take you plan to use.

**Music videos.** Cut a rough sequence against the track before ordering long takes. Use the music to set shot length and put generation credits into the few moments that need difficult motion. For recurring characters, keep an approved reference for each angle you need. Check faces, wardrobe and props across consecutive shots.

**API production.** In n8n, record the brief, reference version and requested model before submitting a generation job. Save its provider job ID and check that job before retrying after a timeout. Copy finished clips and their shot metadata into your own storage. Keep approval ahead of any delivery or publishing action so a retry can never put a rejected take into circulation. ## FAQ

### Can I still use Sora for AI video generation?

OpenAI discontinued the Sora web experience and app on April 26, 2026, and the API on September 24, 2026. Existing production workflows need another generation route.

### Which AI video models generate audio?

Veo 3.1 supports native audio across its model tiers. Kling audio support and billing depend on the version and platform. For example, Runway Dev documents optional audio for Kling 3.0 Standard and audio in Kling 4.0 Flash outputs.

### How should I compare AI video generation costs?

Divide the amount you actually paid for generation and paid processing by the seconds you can use after review and trimming. For a project budget, include editorial time and any subscription minimum you have to cover.

### What keeps a character consistent across AI-generated clips?

Reuse approved references for the angles you need. Keep key details such as wardrobe and props in the shot brief, then check the first, middle and last frames of each take and the cut between them.
