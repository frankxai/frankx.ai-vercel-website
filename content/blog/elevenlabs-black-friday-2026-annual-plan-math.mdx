## Three Questions Before You Lock Creator ($99) or Pro ($445.50)

### 1. Are you using voice cloning or multi-speaker dubbing?

If you rely on a trained studio voice clone with consistent emotional pacing, local open-weight models still take significant tuning and GPU setup to match. At **$8.25/month** effective Year-1 cost, Creator pays for itself in a single client deliverable or two narrated video essays.

### 2. Are you wasting paid characters on rough script drafts?

A common mistake is synthesizing full articles on every minor draft edit. Run draft listens through a fast local TTS engine or `gpt-4o-mini-tts` while editing prose, and send **only final locked scripts** through ElevenLabs. That habit typically cuts monthly character burn by 60–75%.
