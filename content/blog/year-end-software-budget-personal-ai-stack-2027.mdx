## FAQ

### What is the best use of an expiring $1,500–$2,500 L&D budget in December 2026?

Split it between one primary daily workflow tool subscription you use every week and one hands-on technical program or architecture workshop that produces a working repo, evaluation suite, or internal team teardown.

### Should solo founders prepay software in December for tax purposes?

Only prepay tools you would keep paying monthly through next autumn anyway. Locking an annual subscription on an unproven tool saves 20% in taxes while wasting 80% of the cash.

### Can companies expense AI toolkits and workshops with VAT invoices?

Yes, provided the vendor issues a formal VAT-compliant invoice showing entity details and tax registration. See the [FrankX Legal Imprint](/legal/imprint) for current Eenmanszaak entity details.
