**TL;DR:** Google shipped [Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) on 13 August 2026, three weeks after 3.6 Flash. Tulsee Doshi's post calls it the most intelligent Flash workhorse yet for coding and agents, at half the original 3.6 list price through year-end. The benches below are Google's. They are not a FrankX arena receipt. If you need a $20 chat-app pick, stay on [ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini 2026](/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-vs-gemini-2026). ## What did Google ship on 13 August 2026?

A Flash-series update. Not a new Pro. Not a new Ultra. The [Google blog post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) is the primary source.

Google says the jump came from developer feedback and algorithmic work, not a from-scratch train. It claims gains in software engineering, knowledge work, and web-development workflows. It also says 3.7 Flash is the model behind Gemini Spark for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the countries where Spark is available. Spark is excluded from the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom on the support page Google links.

Safety language on that same post: updated CBRN and cyber-offense safeguards, plus a [model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-3-7-flash). I have not audited those controls.

## How much does Gemini 3.7 Flash cost?

Google's own footnote, not a reseller card:

| Window | Input / 1M tokens | Output / 1M tokens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory, through 31 December 2026 | $0.75 | $3.75 |
| From 1 January 2027 | $1.50 | $7.50 |

Google describes the intro rate as half the original 3.6 Flash cost per million tokens. If your billing path is AGY, Vertex, or a gateway, trust that invoice. The table is the public API list price from the 13 August post.

## Should you switch coding agents to Gemini 3.7 Flash this week?

Only if your work already sits in Google's stack, or you have a cheap AGY/API path and a task that looks like Google's benches: debug a repo, generate a UI from a screenshot, grind a long PDF.

Do not switch if you need a judged comparison against Claude Code, Codex, or Grok Build. That write-up is still the [2026 coding-agent guide](/blog/ultimate-guide-ai-coding-agents-2026). Do not switch because a dashboard called it the August winner. That dashboard was lying until the honesty patch landed.

A one-week test that is fair:

1. Pick one closed ticket in a real repo.
2. Run it on your current harness and on AGY `gemini-3.7-flash-high`.
3. Keep the diff, the command, and the wall-clock. That is a receipt. A greeting is not.

## FAQ

### Is Gemini 3.7 Flash the same as Gemini 3.5 Pro?

No. Google shipped a Flash update. The 13 August post does not replace Pro. Axios also framed it as Flash arriving before a 3.5 Pro refresh. Use the model ID your vendor lists.

### Is the $0.75 / $3.75 price permanent?

No. Google's footnote ends the intro price on 31 December 2026. 1 January 2027 is $1.50 / $7.50.

### Does Gemini Spark use 3.7 Flash?

Google says Spark on Google AI Pro and Ultra started using 3.7 Flash on 13 August 2026, except in regions Spark does not support. That is a consumer-agent path, not the coding CLI.

### Can I treat DeepSWE 65.3% as our score?

No. That is Google's published comparison to 3.6 Flash. Copy it with attribution. Do not put `judged: true` on it.

### Did AGY actually answer with Gemini 3.7 tonight?

It identified itself as Gemini 3.7 Flash High Reasoning. It did not execute the print prompt. Recorded as listed-and-greeted, not headless-verified.
