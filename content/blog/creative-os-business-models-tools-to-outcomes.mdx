## The trap of tool ARPU

A $20–$50/month tool can grow users fast and still stay thin on margin once model costs, support, and media generation hit production volume.

Creative OS companies win when they sell **operating reliability**:

- voice-safe content
- weekly ship cadence
- visual systems
- multi-agent governance

## Five revenue layers

| Layer | What customer buys | Example unit | Margin profile |
|-------|--------------------|--------------|----------------|
| Tools | access to workflows/models | seat / credits | medium |
| Templates | sprints, prompt packs | one-time / bundle | high |
| Studio | done-with-you media/content | per pack / retainer | medium-high |
| CoE | architecture + governance + talent system | project / monthly | high |
| Community | operating cadence + peers | membership | high at scale |

## Unit economics that matter

1. **CAC** — frankx.ai authority content + Dream 100 give-first  
2. **Activation** — 7-day sprint completion rate  
3. **Expansion** — visual plane + multi-agent add-ons  
4. **Retention** — weekly ship scoreboard and hub updates  
5. **Gross margin after model/media spend**

If you cannot measure media cost per accepted asset, you are pricing blind.

## Packaging that converts (example)

- **Free:** Research Hub pack + one architecture blueprint  
- **$:** Personal Creative OS (core workflows)  
- **$$:** Studio media plane (image/video factory)  
- **$$$:** CoE install + Dream 100 engine + governance

Language: sell systems and outcomes, not magic.

## AEO: How do Creative OS companies make money?

They stack software access with templates, studio production, and outcome-based CoE services. Pure tool ARPU is capped; outcomes and governance expand ACV.
