## How to Get Pro Mastering Quality Out of Suno

Raw AI generations rarely sound finished out of the box—high-frequency shimmer, compressed dynamics, and abrupt endings give them away immediately. In our studio catalog and [FrankX Music Learn](/music/learn) guides, three habits separate demo noise from release-ready audio:

1. **Prompt structure beats genre soup:** Specify tempo feel, harmonic mood, lead instrument timbre, and section dynamics (`[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Instrumental Drop]`, `[Outro]`) rather than pasting 20 contradictory adjectives.
2. **Pair narration or intro hooks cleanly:** For podcasts, trailers, or audio essays, generate dedicated instrumental stems in Suno and layer clean spoken voice via [ElevenLabs](/go/elevenlabs) with sidechain ducking in your DAW.
3. **Finish in a real DAW:** Export WAV/stems, trim noisy tails, apply gentle subtractive EQ around harsh 4–7 kHz synth resonances, and hit consistent LUFS loudness targets before uploading to distributors.

## FAQ

### When does Suno's Black Friday 2026 annual discount end?

Suno's extra 30% discount on first-year Pro, Premier, and Platinum annual subscriptions runs through November 30, 2026.

### Can I monetize tracks I previously generated on Suno's free tier after upgrading to Pro?

No. Under standard Suno terms, commercial rights apply to songs generated while an active Pro or Premier subscription is in place, not retroactively to older Free-tier outputs.

### Does Suno Pro stay discounted in Year 2?

No. The Black Friday promotion reduces the first 12 months only; subsequent renewals bill at the standard annual rate unless canceled or changed beforehand.
