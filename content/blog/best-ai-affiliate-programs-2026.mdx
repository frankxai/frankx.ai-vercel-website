Start with a tutorial you would publish anyway. A creator reading your voiceover guide might need to try ElevenLabs. A developer following your web-data guide might need to run an Apify Actor. Both readers have a specific reason to click. That reason is worth more than a place at the top of a commission table.

## Which AI affiliate program fits your audience?

These are published program terms checked on 24 November 2026. The rates describe what a program offers. Your agreement, accepted markets and actual payouts may differ. The table does not rank audio, video, data and funnel software against each other.

Public enrollment and a live referral are different milestones. Before using a tracked link, open your account, confirm your channel is allowed, use the link the provider issued, and verify the destination. Keep ordinary vendor links when they help the reader complete the work.

## When does recurring commission pay more?

Take a $30 monthly plan. A 50% commission on the first payment earns $15. A 30% recurring commission earns $9 per paid month. The recurring deal overtakes it after the second paid month, assuming both referrals qualify and neither payment is refunded.

| Paid months | One-time commission | Recurring commission |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | $15 | $9 |
| 2 | $15 | $18 |
| 6 | $15 | $54 |
| 12 | $15 | $108 |

Treat this as a worksheet, with no assumption about your conversion rate or retention. Start with outbound clicks, estimate the share that becomes eligible paying customers, then multiply by the commission per paid month and expected paid months. Apply caps and refunds before estimating approved commission. Track cash separately because the payout can arrive much later.

A higher percentage is easy to copy into a spreadsheet. The difficult inputs are whether the reader needs the tool, whether the buyer qualifies and whether they stay. When you have little data, write those three assumptions down and use the next thirty days to see which one is weakest.

## How do you make the recommendation worth a click?

Give the reader one decision they can make with the evidence on the page. For a voice workflow, use the same script across providers and show where the pronunciation or editing time changes the outcome. For video, price the shots you can actually use. For web data, validate the result against a small fixture before you recommend scheduling it.

The interface matters too. A tool being callable through MCP tells you how a client can access it. The useful questions are what the tool returns, what happens when it fails, how the run is billed and whether you can export the output.
