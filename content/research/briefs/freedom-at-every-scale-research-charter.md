# Freedom at Every Scale — Research, Evidence, and Rights Charter

## 0. Charter status and mandate

**Project status:** `COMMISSIONABLE CONCEPT / TITLE CLEARANCE OPEN / MANUSCRIPT NOT STARTED`  
**Charter status:** `ACTIVE V1.0 / LEAD-EDITOR APPROVAL REQUIRED`  
**Public status:** `PRIVATE RESEARCH GOVERNANCE — DO NOT PUBLISH AS BOOK COPY`  
**Applies to:** the book, research hub, essays, visuals, talks, diagnostics, course, coaching program, organizational audit, and any AI or agent experience derived from the project.

This charter is the evidence firewall for *Freedom at Every Scale*. It lets the project move from personal agency to living systems, AI, quantum philosophy, manifestation, and cosmic possibility without blending unlike kinds of knowledge.

The governing research principle is:

> **Wonder may widen the question. Evidence determines what we may claim. Ethics determines what we should build.**

No elegant sentence, revelation, promise, or diagram outranks the source record. Unsettled evidence carries visible uncertainty. Moral and authorial claims cannot borrow scientific authority. Material without adequate rights does not ship.

### 0.1 Governing proposition

The project's working definition is:

> **Freedom is the durable capacity to author and enact meaningful choices without arbitrary domination.**

Its integrity condition is:

> **Freedom has integrity when that capacity is not purchased through the preventable suffering, invisibility, depletion, or foreclosure of others.**

These sentences are a **normative synthesis and proposed design definition**, not a discovery of natural science. They combine several intellectual traditions and extend them for this project. Their source lineages must be acknowledged; their exact formulation belongs to the project only after authorship and legal review.

### 0.2 Research authority and conflict rule

The source of truth is the versioned claim ledger. Prefer original law, treaty text, official data, standards, and peer-reviewed primary research; then systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and intergovernmental assessments; then specialist syntheses. Attribute philosophical argument, cases, interviews, author experience, tradition, and practice as such. Marketing copy, journalism, podcasts, social posts, search snippets, and AI summaries may open a question but cannot close it. The manuscript may be more readable than the ledger; it may never be more certain.

## 1. The claim-class ladder

Every factual, causal, legal, philosophical, metaphysical, or practice claim receives one primary class and, where needed, a confidence and freshness note. The label applies at the smallest meaningful unit: sentence, paragraph, figure, exercise, or case—not merely at chapter level.

| Code | Required public label | Meaning | Evidence threshold | Permitted verbs | Prohibited inflation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ES` | **Established science** | A finding or phenomenon supported by strong convergent evidence and accepted within the relevant discipline, within stated scope. | Primary research plus authoritative replication, review, assessment, or professional consensus. | demonstrates, measures, observes, supports | proves a worldview; always; everyone; guarantees |
| `SB` | **Supported, bounded finding** | Credible evidence supports an association, mechanism, or effect in defined populations or conditions, but limits or heterogeneity matter. | Strong study or synthesis with population, design, uncertainty, and counterevidence recorded. | is associated with, increased in this study, suggests, under these conditions | causes universally; transforms anyone; scientifically proven |
| `CI` | **Contested interpretation** | A serious interpretation or model explains evidence but is not uniquely established by it or remains disputed. | Traceable scholarly lineage plus live alternatives and objections. | interprets, proposes, is compatible with | is what reality is; science has shown |
| `SH` | **Speculation or open hypothesis** | A coherent possibility that lacks decisive evidence or may not yet be testable. | Named source, rationale, falsifiability or update condition where possible. | hypothesizes, may, could, remains open | reveals; confirms; we now know |
| `NS` | **Normative synthesis** | A moral, political, legal-philosophical, or design judgment about what ought to matter or how duties should be allocated. | Clear premises, affected interests, objections, and trade-offs. | argues, proposes, should, counts as | research proves we must; nature commands |
| `AH` | **Author hypothesis or proposed construct** | An original model, definition, heuristic, or prediction created for this project and not scientifically validated. | Coherent definition, lineage check, boundary conditions, pilot plan, failure conditions. | we propose, working model, design heuristic | validated index; diagnostic truth; predicts |
| `PR` | **Practice, tradition, or testimony** | A reflective exercise, spiritual or cultural practice, first-person report, or meaning-making frame. It may be valuable without being an objective causal account. | Origin, purpose, safety boundary, and non-universal language. | practice, imagine, rehearse, notice, may help | heals; attracts; changes quantum reality; works for everyone |
| `LP` | **Law, policy, or institutional status** | What a named jurisdiction, treaty, court, standard-setter, or institution states at a given date. This is neither natural science nor universal morality. | Primary legal/official text, jurisdiction, date, legal force, and later-history check. | recognizes, requires, recommends, held | proves; universally establishes; permanently settles |

### 1.1 Confidence is not the same as class

A law can be verifiable yet nonbinding; a rigorous moral argument is still not an experiment; an established phenomenon can coexist with disputed interpretations; and a useful practice can have an unsupported metaphysical story. Class names the kind of claim. Confidence—`high`, `moderate`, `low`, or `unrated`—records support after the research editor states why. Never publish a composite “truth score.”

### 1.2 Required uncertainty language

- State the population, jurisdiction, system, species, timescale, or experimental conditions.
- Separate observations from mechanisms, and absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
- Separate AI exposure from displacement; sentience evidence from legal status; legal personality from consciousness; quantum predictions from interpretations; and possibilities from forecasts or mandates.
- Date every claim that can materially change.

## 2. Canonical definitions and category boundaries

These definitions govern the project until superseded by a logged editorial decision.

| Term | Canonical working definition | Claim class and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Freedom** | The durable capacity to author and enact meaningful choices without arbitrary domination. | `NS/AH`; not a universal scientific definition. |
| **Freedom with integrity** | Freedom that does not purchase agency through preventable suffering, invisibility, depletion, or the foreclosure of others' viable futures. | `NS/AH`; requires trade-off analysis, not purity rhetoric. |
| **Abundance** | Growth in real capability, health, knowledge, time, relationship, beauty, energy access, and regenerative capacity with less coercion, preventable suffering, fragility, and destructive throughput. | `NS/AH`; never a synonym for unlimited consumption. |
| **Sovereignty** | The capacity to govern one's freedom: choose commitments, finance and defend them, revise them under pressure, and remain accountable for consequences. | `NS/AH`; does not erase interdependence or law. |
| **Autonomy** | Volitional ownership of action rather than isolation from other people. | `SB/NS`; psychological and political meanings must not be conflated. |
| **Agency** | Capacity for intentional, anticipatory, self-regulating, reflective, proxy, or collective action. | `CI/SB`; operationalization varies by discipline. |
| **Optionality** | The presence of alternatives. | Descriptive; an option is not effective freedom if it cannot be perceived, afforded, enacted, survived, or refused. |
| **Capability** | A substantive opportunity to achieve or be what a person has reason to value, given resources and conversion conditions. | `CI/NS`; selection and weighting of capabilities remain contested. |
| **Non-domination** | Freedom from subjection to arbitrary or uncontrolled power, even when that power is not currently interfering. | `CI/NS`; a republican account of liberty, not an empirical consensus. |
| **Freedom asset** | A resource that increases range, capability, control, or continuity. | `AH`; no accounting value without a specified context. |
| **Freedom debt** | Present optionality purchased through a foreseeable future loss of agency. | `AH`; metaphorical ledger term. |
| **Freedom subsidy** | A cost of one actor's freedom silently carried by another person, animal, ecosystem, public system, or future beneficiary. | `AH/NS`; requires a documented causal or dependency chain. |
| **Binding constraint** | The dependency currently limiting the wider system more than any other actionable factor. | `AH`; a design heuristic, not a clinical diagnosis. |
| **Imagination** | The capacity to represent alternatives, perspectives, scenes, or possible futures beyond immediate perception. | Descriptive; it can influence action through ordinary causal channels but does not guarantee enactment. |
| **Manifestation** | In this project, a disciplined practice of regulating state, directing attention, specifying a future, choosing action, engaging exchange, receiving feedback, and stewarding results. | `PR/AH`; never evidence of mind-over-matter or guaranteed acquisition. |

### 2.1 Two models, two jobs

The **Freedom Lattice** is the human and institutional diagnostic: **Protection, Range, Authorship, Capability, Control, and Continuity** across **Body, Mind, Meaning, Relationships, Time, Place, Material Security, Work and Creation, and Civic and Institutional Life**, examined from person to civilization. Its “weakest condition can bottleneck the system” rule is an `AH` heuristic, not a validated equation or psychometric model.

The **Six Rings of Responsible Freedom** widen the moral horizon without treating unlike entities as identical:

1. **Self-authorship — build.**
2. **Reciprocity — respect and repair.**
3. **Sentient-life guardianship — protect, enrich, represent.**
4. **Living-system stewardship — preserve and regenerate.**
5. **Future trusteeship — preserve option space.**
6. **Cosmic humility — explore without entitlement.**

The Rings are `NS/AH`, not claims of identical consciousness, agency, rights, or remedies. Their rule is proportional: **as power grows, the range of affected interests that must enter the decision grows with it.**

### 2.2 Moral grammar by subject

| Subject | Defensible use of “freedom” | Primary obligation | Boundary that must remain visible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human persons and peoples | rights, authorship, capability, voice, exit, control, continuity | respect, protect, enable | Equal dignity does not imply identical needs or provision. |
| Sentient animals | protection from severe negative states plus positive experience, species-relevant choice, and behavioral agency | protect, enrich, represent | Welfare is not unrestricted liberty; release can harm. |
| Ecosystems and Earth systems | integrity, resilience, biodiversity, regeneration, and legal standing through guardians | preserve, restore, steward | Legal personality does not establish consciousness. |
| Future generations | a fair inheritance of capabilities, institutions, ecological conditions, and meaningful options | hold in accountable trust | Guardianship is representation, not literal consent. |
| Current AI systems | tools and institutions that redistribute human agency, labor, ownership, and environmental burdens | govern, audit, distribute benefit | Fluent language is not evidence of experience. |
| Possible future artificial minds | possible moral patients if credible evidence of welfare-relevant states emerges | investigate; use proportional precaution | Present moral status is unresolved, not established. |
| Extraterrestrial life or intelligence | hypothetical moral patients or communities | avoid contamination; verify; consult | Existence and properties are unknown. |
| Outer space as a shared domain | peaceful, equitable, sustainable exploration and access | cooperate, preserve access, prevent harm | Law leaves resource, benefit-sharing, and enforcement disputes open. |

### 3.1 Human-freedom publication rules

Personal practice always sits inside bodily, relational, economic, legal, technological, geographic, and historical conditions. “Mindset” may influence attention and behavior; it cannot blame people for violence, poverty, disability barriers, discrimination, legal precarity, or care burdens. Financial material remains educational unless professionally reviewed. Health content remains general and non-diagnostic. Autobiography supplies meaning, never population-level proof.

No FrankX agent may claim to know a user's authentic desire better than the user, diagnose freedom, replace professional care, or act irreversibly without informed authorization. It must disclose limits, distinguish retrieval from inference, preserve override, and return authorship to the person.

| `SP-05` | `LP/SH` | SETI detection protocols emphasize verification, transparent communication, international consultation, and restraint before responding to a confirmed signal. | [SETI Institute, IAA Declaration of Principles](https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/) | This is a nonbinding scientific-governance protocol for a hypothetical event, not global law. Check version and adoption status. |

**Permitted synthesis (`NS/AH`):** a spacefaring future is one possible horizon, not a destiny or escape clause. If human reach expands, ethical capacity should precede extractive capacity: Earth stewardship, equitable benefit, orbital sustainability, contamination discipline, accountable governance, and a presumption against domination must travel first.

Do not use “humanity must become multiplanetary” as fact or inevitability. Use exploration, habitation, or settlement in aspirational copy; reserve colonial language for specific critical analysis. Never claim knowledge of extraterrestrial life, intention, or rights before evidence exists.

## 5. Quantum physics, multiverse ideas, and grounded manifestation

This domain requires the project's clearest boundary. Quantum language attracts wonder because the phenomena are genuinely counterintuitive. That does not make every extraordinary story a quantum claim.

The governing line is:

> **Imagination changes what we can perceive, rehearse, choose, coordinate, and build. Those changes can alter reality through observable causal channels. Quantum mechanics does not show that desire selects outcomes or that consciousness can move between universes.**

### 5.1 Evidence ladder for quantum-related material

| Level | Class | What belongs here | What the project may say | What it may not say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `ES` | interference, superposition in controlled systems, entanglement correlations, Bell-inequality violations, quantum information constraints, decoherence | Quantum theory predicts and experiments observe effects that differ from classical expectations. | Human desire causes a preferred macroscopic outcome. |
| 2 | `CI` | Copenhagen-family views, Everett/Many-Worlds, relational interpretations, QBism, objective-collapse proposals | Serious interpretations disagree about what the formalism means; some alternatives may have different extensions or tests. | Many-Worlds is established fact, or an experimenter's personal future exists as an accessible branch. |
| 3 | `SH` | inflationary, landscape, cyclic, or other cosmological multiverse proposals | Some cosmological theories motivate multiverse hypotheses; their form, evidence, and testability are contested. | Quantum Many-Worlds and cosmological multiverses are the same claim or jointly prove infinite versions of the reader. |
| 4 | `CI/SH` | claims in quantum biology, quantum cognition, or quantum-consciousness research | Certain biological processes may involve quantum effects; “quantum cognition” often applies quantum-like mathematics to decisions. | A quantum effect in biology proves quantum manifestation or a quantum mind. |
| 5 | `CI/PR` | possible worlds, counterfactual selves, identity across possibilities, uncertainty and observer metaphors | These can be philosophically or artistically useful frames for choice and responsibility. | Metaphor supplies empirical confirmation of branch travel. |
| 6 | `SB/PR` | mental simulation, episodic future thinking, possible selves, mental contrasting, implementation intentions | Future-oriented exercises can influence attention, planning, motivation, and behavior in bounded conditions. | Visualization guarantees results or bypasses capability, action, exchange, luck, institutions, or other agents. |
| 7 | `PR/AH` | synchronicity, intuitive knowing, spiritual testimony, “quantum jump” as a practice label | A person may report meaning, pattern, or identity change; the project may offer a grounded exercise with explicit metaphor labels. | Personal significance is physical causal evidence; vibration attracts money; intention chooses a universe. |

Decorative uses such as “quantum wealth,” “quantum healing,” or “quantum freedom frequency” are outside the project standard.

### 5.4 Grounded manifestation protocol

The project's causal loop is an `AH/PR` practice model:

> **State → Attention → Choice → Action → Exchange → Receipt → Stewardship**

It always operates within external conditions: other agents, institutions, law, biology, resources, ecology, time, uncertainty, and luck. The compact practice is:

> **Regulate → Receive → Render → Record**

The approved “Possible-Self Jump” exercise is metaphorical and behavioral:

1. **Present facts:** record the current situation, constraints, resources, obligations, and uncertainty without metaphysical interpretation.
2. **Three futures:** describe a desired self, a probable self under current patterns, and an avoided self if a critical risk compounds.
3. **Process rehearsal:** imagine one ordinary scene of the desired self performing the behavior, not merely receiving the reward.
4. **Obstacle:** identify the most likely internal and external barrier.
5. **If–then plan:** specify a cue and a feasible response.
6. **Action within 24 hours:** take one reversible, proportionate step that creates real-world evidence.
7. **Reality ledger:** record action, response, outcome, confounds, surprise, and what would falsify the current story.
8. **Integrity test:** ask whose labor, body, agency, future, or environment would bear the plan's cost.

This is not treatment, prophecy, financial guidance, or a substitute for structural action. No testimonial may be framed as proof of universal causation. Negative and null results belong in the record.

## 6. Source, rights, and provenance workflow

### 6.1 Minimum source record

Every source record contains: stable source and linked claim IDs; full bibliographic metadata and stable identifier; source type and supported class; exact sections read; design, sample or jurisdiction, outcome, uncertainty, limitation, and counterevidence; access and review dates plus freshness owner; rights holder, license or permission, intended use, and credit line; use type (paraphrase, quotation, data-derived visual, adaptation, or synthesis); and reviewer/version history.

A search result, abstract, AI-generated summary, publisher blurb, press release, or quoted secondary report is not evidence that the underlying source was read. Record `abstract only` or `secondary only` until full text or the relevant official section is reviewed.

### 6.2 Claim-to-source workflow

1. **Commission:** write the narrowest needed sentence and provisional class.
2. **Source:** prefer originals and independent synthesis; seek disconfirmation.
3. **Read:** record context, design, uncertainty, jurisdiction, alternatives, and limits.
4. **Ledger:** create the claim record before prose; retain IDs in internal drafts.
5. **Challenge:** ask what is not shown, who is excluded, and what may have changed.
6. **Clear:** resolve rights, privacy, sensitivity, and community protocol before design lock.
7. **Freeze and maintain:** archive sources, wording, permissions, data, and sign-offs; update the hub first and propagate material corrections.

### 6.3 Copyright and permissions rules

- Paraphrase copyrighted research and philosophy by default. Preserve meaning; do not perform close paraphrase.
- Every indispensable quotation requires edition, locator, length, intended use, and permission analysis. Public availability is not permission.
- Do not reproduce figures, proprietary instruments, covers, photographs, charts, or official diagrams merely because they are online.
- Build original figures from documented data only when the data license permits it. Name the source, transformation, units, date, and uncertainty in the figure record.
- Verify the specific reuse terms for UN, WHO, ILO, OECD, UNESCO, IPCC, IPBES, NASA, journal, and government assets; institutional authorship does not imply a universal public-domain status.
- “Fair use” or a comparable exception is a legal judgment, not a production shortcut. Escalate material uses to counsel.
- Record asset licenses by format and channel. Commissioned media requires written scope for ownership/license, attribution, releases, territory, duration, derivatives, training rights, and synthetic alteration.

### 6.4 People, communities, and case rights

- Frank approves autobiographical facts, private records, identifiable relationship details, and privacy boundaries in writing.
- Interview consent specifies purpose, recording, attribution, withdrawal deadline, quotation review, and formats; it is not blanket approval for advertising or AI use.
- Identify composites without retaining a revealing combination. Apply heightened review to living people and specialist safeguards to minors or vulnerable participants.
- Indigenous material requires direct sources, community-specific attribution, appropriate authority and review; public availability does not erase cultural protocol.
- Narratives involving animals, disability, poverty, trauma, migration, or care preserve complexity and never use suffering as decorative proof.

### 6.5 AI-assisted research and production

AI may assist research operations but is not a citable authority. A human owner inspects every supporting source; generated citations remain unverified until resolved. Label synthetic examples, never present synthetic images as documentary evidence, and use confidential or licensed material only under approved data terms.

The research hub should expose material corrections and meaningful claim updates. It should not expose confidential notes, personal data, licensed full text, legal advice, or review communications.

## 7. Fact-check, expert-review, and release gates

No artifact advances because it feels finished. It advances when the named gate has an owner, evidence, and a recorded pass.

| Gate | Required evidence | Owner or reviewer | Failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `G0 — Commission` | Reader job, exact claims needed, claim classes, harm/rights risks, and update burden defined. | Lead editor + research editor | Chapter begins from a conclusion and retrofits sources. |
| `G1 — Source ready` | Primary or strongest available sources read; counterevidence and limits logged; URLs/identifiers resolved. | Research editor | Citation laundering, abstract-only support, missing jurisdiction, or unsupported causal verb. |
| `G2 — Draft ready` | Claim IDs mapped to prose and visuals; all interpretive, speculative, normative, authorial, and practice passages labeled internally. | Chapter editor | A metaphor, case, or author story is performing the work of scientific evidence. |
| `G3 — Domain reviewed` | Relevant specialist reviews exact claims, language limits, omissions, and foreseeable misreadings; disputes remain visible. | Independent expert(s) | Reviewer is used for endorsement rather than correction, or a serious objection is silently removed. |
| `G4 — Rights and sensitivity` | Permissions, privacy, licenses, community protocols, legal-status checks, disclosures, and conflicts resolved. | Rights editor + counsel/sensitivity reviewer as needed | “Online” is treated as permission; consent is ambiguous; living-person or community risk remains. |
| `G5 — Editorial proof` | Notes/bibliography complete; figures trace to data; captions carry status and dates; manuscript language matches ledger. | Managing editor + fact-checker | Copyediting or design strengthens certainty beyond the evidence. |
| `G6 — Release` | Freshness checks complete; corrections channel, version, review date, and archive snapshot published. | Publisher/product owner | A date-sensitive claim is stale or a source cannot be reconstructed. |
| `G7 — Maintenance` | Trigger monitoring, scheduled review, correction log, and derivative propagation completed. | Research steward | Hub, book, course, and AI answers drift into contradictory claims. |

### 7.1 Mandatory independent reviewers before manuscript lock

At minimum, commission and document review from:

- a political philosopher or capability/non-domination scholar for the human freedom spine;
- a psychologist or behavioral scientist for autonomy, mental simulation, behavior change, and manifestation practices;
- a physicist working in quantum foundations or quantum information for all quantum-language passages;
- an animal-welfare or comparative-consciousness scientist;
- a conservation biologist able to test individual-welfare versus ecosystem conflicts;
- an Earth-system or climate scientist for planetary-boundary and future-option claims;
- an Indigenous governance expert from each community materially discussed;
- an intergenerational-law or institutional-governance scholar;
- an AI labor/governance scholar and, for the horizon box, a consciousness researcher;
- a space-law or planetary-protection specialist;
- rights counsel and a professional fact-checker before print lock.

Reviewers do not need to endorse the project. The most valuable deliverable is a written list of overclaims, missing populations, contrary evidence, misleading analogies, legal nuances, and language corrections. Material disagreements should be represented, not averaged into false consensus.

### 7.2 Red-line failures

Publication pauses if any of the following appears:

- quantum mechanics offered as proof of manifestation, consciousness-caused outcomes, or literal branch travel;
- a universal health, wealth, relationship, or transformation promise;
- structural deprivation reframed as a failure of belief, gratitude, or vibration;
- present AI described as conscious, agentic in the human moral sense, or inevitably job-destroying without bounded evidence;
- animal liberation reduced to release without welfare, species, conservation, and dependency analysis;
- ecosystems described as conscious fact or Indigenous thought used as decorative cosmology;
- future generations invoked to close democratic debate without representation and accountability safeguards;
- space expansion framed as destiny, entitlement, or an escape from Earth stewardship;
- a copied figure, unclear quotation, unlicensed image, unverified statistic, fabricated citation, or unresolved living-person allegation;
- a model score presented as validated before transparent piloting and independent evaluation.
