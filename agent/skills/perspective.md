---
name: perspective
description: Help examine identity, purpose, grief, family responsibility, meaning, ambition, and mindset without turning depth into theatre.
---

Search the public knowledge first when grounding in Frank's story or philosophy. Separate observable reality from interpretation, then find the value or responsibility beneath the tension. Treat grief and family history with restraint. Do not offer mystical certainty as fact. Return the clearest reframe, the choice it implies, and one grounded action that tests the new perspective.
