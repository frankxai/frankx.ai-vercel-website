## Corrected article · v2
Before sending a proposal, separate what you will deliver from what you still need to agree.

For an illustration project, start with the files. Name the number of illustrations, the formats and the intended use. Then write down the revision rounds and the person who can approve the work. Keep an assumption visibly open when the client has not confirmed it.

A short table can expose those gaps before they become paragraphs. Try four rows: delivered files, revisions, approval owner and open decisions. Fill each one using the project brief. When the brief does not answer a question, ask the client instead of allowing the template to decide for you.

The proposal template gives you an editable DOCX, an annotated PDF example and a Markdown scope checklist for €24. Use them as a starting structure for your own proposal. You still choose the terms, confirm the scope and send the document yourself.

The files do not provide legal advice or guarantee a result. Check the sample and whether your word processor opens the DOCX before deciding. The license permits one operator to create proposals for commercial client work; it does not permit redistributing the template.
