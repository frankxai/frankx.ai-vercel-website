## Corrected email · v2
Subject: Turn one finished project into a case-study draft

Choose a project you can discuss without revealing a client's private information. Before writing the post, make three short notes: the starting problem, the work you actually did, and the outcome you can substantiate.

Keep the last note modest when the evidence is modest. A working prototype or a completed handover can be worth describing. You do not need to turn it into a revenue claim.

In the case-study writing workshop on 12 November, we will use those notes to draft a post and review it against a checklist. The session runs online from 14:00 to 15:30 CET. Bring one anonymised project and any evidence you have permission to use.

The workshop costs €75 including applicable VAT in this example. The session includes drafting time and the review checklist. It does not include publishing, ongoing editing or a promise of customer leads.

Reply for the registration details. I will send the scope and terms so you can decide whether it fits the work you have ready.

### Offer paragraph
Bring one anonymised project to a 90-minute online case-study writing workshop. We will separate the problem, your actual work and the outcome you can support, then draft a post and review it with a checklist. The session is scheduled for 12 November 2026, 14:00–15:30 CET, at €75 including applicable VAT in this fictional offer. Publishing and ongoing editing are excluded. Request the registration details before purchasing.

### Acceptance
C1 and C2 retained within exercise scope; C3/C4 removed. Final human approval pending. Actual registration URL, current capacity and terms remain operator checks. Do not send this fictional event invitation to a real audience.
