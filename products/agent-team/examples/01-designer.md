# Worked example 1 · a brand designer's existing review offer
Fictional teaching case. No customer, revenue, research interview or market result is asserted. These are authored reference outputs, not transcripts of a ChatGPT or Claude test.

## Input: paste only this section for a practice run
Mara is a freelance brand designer serving solo consultants. Her existing Homepage Clarity Review is €180 for an annotated homepage PDF and a 20-minute recorded walkthrough, delivered within five working days. It excludes implementation, logo design and ongoing consulting. Her goal is one useful LinkedIn post connecting a homepage diagnosis to that offer. Available time: 120 minutes. Use a warm, direct voice; avoid revenue promises. CTA: invite the reader to request the scope by reply; no invented booking link.

## Corrected publication package · v2
### Post
Before rewriting your homepage, try reading only the opening screen.

Write down three answers: who the service is for, what the person will receive, and what they should do next. Use the words already on the page. When you need to explain an answer from memory, mark that sentence for another pass.

This is where I start a homepage review. I want to see whether the page carries the explanation you would give in a conversation. A line such as “strategic support for ambitious businesses” leaves me looking for the service. “A homepage review for solo consultants” gives me something I can evaluate.

Then I look at the next action. Does the button explain what happens after the click? Are you asking someone to book, buy or send a question? Choose the action you can actually follow up on this week.

You can use those questions on your own page today. For a second reading, my Homepage Clarity Review covers one homepage. You receive an annotated PDF and a 20-minute recorded walkthrough within five working days, for €180. Implementation is outside the scope.

Reply “scope” and I will send the details.

### Offer paragraph
The Homepage Clarity Review covers the opening message and next action on one homepage. For €180, you receive an annotated PDF and a 20-minute recorded walkthrough within five working days. The review uses your current page and offer. It includes specific wording observations and recommended edits. Implementation, logo design and ongoing consulting are outside the scope. Reply “scope” to request the details before deciding.
