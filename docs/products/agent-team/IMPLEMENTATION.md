## Experience thesis and three directions
Recipient: a founder who already uses a chat account and wants one finished package this week. Primary action: inspect the worked example. Signature proof: compare an unsupported claim to its corrected offer wording. Feeling: an inspectable working document.

1. Role gallery: five large role portraits, role-first navigation, display typography and animated handoffs. Rejected: it emphasizes the team metaphor before the actual output and adds decorative assets.
2. Working document: restrained typographic hierarchy, real text artifacts, before/after evidence and native disclosure controls. Selected: readers can judge the method and copy the sample without imagining unbuilt automation. No authored motion.
3. Guided workspace: stepper, editable brief and stage-by-stage reveal on the public site. Rejected for this release: it could imply hosted execution and would duplicate the offline guide. The context form belongs in the portable package.
