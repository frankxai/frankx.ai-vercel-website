## “Superfan” editorial contract

Superfans are earned through recognition and usefulness rather than simulated intimacy.

- **Recognition:** name the real pressure before teaching the model.
- **Continuity:** reuse a coherent worldview, vocabulary, cast, and set of operating questions across articles.
- **Receipts:** show sources, boundaries, rejected claims, and what the reader can verify.
- **Agency:** give the reader a decision, test, or implementation path they can use immediately.
- **Return value:** connect each article to the next problem in the reader's journey instead of adding generic calls to action.
- **Human stakes:** make room for time, trust, craft, relationships, responsibility, and the cost of getting the system wrong.

## Release stop conditions

- Missing or fallback hero in the preview.
- A logo generated inside a scene, modified, or presented as a partner without proof.
- Broken mobile layout, unreadable mark grid, layout shift, or missing alt text.
- Failed MDX, content, type, build, internal-link, media, or preview check.
- New claims unsupported by a primary source where the claim is technical, commercial, legal, or time-sensitive.
- A process note that implies a tool, model, or reviewer was used when it was not.
