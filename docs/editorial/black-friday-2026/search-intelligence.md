### Title

The blog page renders `<title>` as `post.title + " | FrankX"` (`app/layout.tsx` template `%s | ${siteConfig.shortName}`, `lib/seo.ts` shortName `FrankX`, and `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` sets `title: post.title`). The suffix adds 9 characters, and the frontmatter title is also the H1.

| Option | Frontmatter chars | Rendered chars | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Higgsfield and ElevenLabs Black Friday 2026 deals** | 49 | 58 | Recommended. Both brands first, fits with the suffix. |
| Black Friday 2026: Higgsfield and ElevenLabs deals | 50 | 59 | Alternative |
| Black Friday 2026: why I'm buying Higgsfield and ElevenLabs (current) | 59 | 68 | Over 60 when rendered; Google will likely truncate the suffix only, but the brands sit after the date |

If the author wants to keep the first-person H1, the blog route has no separate SEO title field today, so the H1 and title must match. That is a code change and outside this brief.

### Description

**Higgsfield Black Friday 2026: up to 69% off annual plans and ElevenLabs Creator at $99 for the first year. Prices, cost per output, and other AI deals.** (151 characters)

Both use only figures already stated in the draft. Do not write a fixed Higgsfield end date: the draft says the plan offer shows a countdown, not a date.
