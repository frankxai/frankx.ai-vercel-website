# FrankX blog quality bar (30 Aug 2026)

Use on every frankx.ai article. Gold sample on main: `content/blog/gemini-3-7-flash-what-google-shipped-august-2026.mdx`.

## Thinking
- Label vendor copy vs observed vs measured. No bake-off without a receipt.
- Kill internal workshop voice. Write for a founder who already pays for one model.
- Humanizer: no "it's not X, it's Y", no "dadada xxx, not xxx", no banned taste.md phrases.
- One quiet field note naming harness/skill is enough for Superfans. No badge wall.

## Media
- Heroes: native `image_generate`, 16:9, no readable text, no vendor logos, no people, no orbit/node clichés. Inspect the file. Compress to WebP under 300KB.
- Infographics: SVG with labels in type. Inline official marks from `public/images/logos/{anthropic,openai,google,xai,...}.svg`. Never generate a partner wordmark.
- Skip custom/generated marks (Hermes caduceus, Antigravity orbits, NVIDIA spiral) unless legal confirms they are official.

## Ship
- Isolated worktree. Draft PR. Merge stays human.
