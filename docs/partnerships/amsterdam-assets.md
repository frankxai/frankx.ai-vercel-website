The photos are distinct originals. A closer Reguliersgracht view from the same photographer and a separate Entrepotdok photograph were inspected and left out of the edition. The two selected sources were resized and encoded once, then served through Next Image with responsive sizes and fixed intrinsic proportions. Next handles delivery caching.

Cloudflare’s three media buckets were queried for `amsterdam`, `frankx/amsterdam` and `frankx/` key prefixes. The tested prefixes returned no matching objects. The Visual Studio governance page and VIS #71 described private candidate source custody. Drive and the founder-city registry showed contextual files, with event photo rights and likeness approvals unresolved. Figma and Canva provided design references. No exact, rights-cleared meetup or campus photography was found in that bounded search.
