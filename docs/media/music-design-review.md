# Music experience review

September 10, 2026. Independent design/accessibility and music-system source
reviews followed the verified-audio repair in PR #666 and isolated homepage
fallback in PR #669. This is an audit and bounded usability correction, not a
completed visual redesign or production approval.

## Evidence and boundaries

The September 9 browser check established audible-source readiness, advancing
time, pause/resume, seeking, track switching and client-navigation continuity on
the repaired preview. All 61 existing published MP3 exports passed full-byte,
SHA-256, decoder and range checks. This does not establish a complete Suno backup.

The September 10 review could read GitHub source and Vercel deployment records.
Fresh visual capture was blocked by Cloud Browser URL security policy. The
browser explicitly prohibited alternate URLs, browser surfaces or execution
paths to obtain the blocked page. This is an environment restriction, not proof
that the deployment is unavailable to visitors. Current desktop/mobile,
screen-reader and physical-device verification remain outstanding.

The governing AGENTS.md and world-class-web-release skill require current
desktop and mobile captures before visual ideation. No composition direction,
typography replacement, artwork publication or motion redesign is approved by
this source audit.

The audio element, verified-source eligibility, gesture-bound playback and
navigation lifecycle are retained. Browsing and suggestions do not select,
restart or replace the current song. Metadata matching is not an analysis of
the audio and does not establish cognitive or conversion benefits.

## Asset and editorial findings

The 61 verified audio exports do not have verified owned covers in the existing
registry. Their cover URLs and matching inventory images hotlink Suno. The
reviewed local Star Show Us cover is available, while that track's audio still
needs an official export. Do not apply its cover to unrelated recordings.

The five album records remain drafts, without verified Spotify album URLs.
Preserve that distinction. Existing listening collections, music education,
tools and production pages can supply context; their existence is not proof of
an approved release, rights grant or conversion result.

Raw genre strings can contain long Suno production prompts. Do not display them
as polished dock subtitles. Compact listener-facing descriptors and artwork
require a reviewed public projection. Do not invent mood metadata: the current
inventory has no dedicated mood field.
