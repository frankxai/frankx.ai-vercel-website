# FrankX solar field guide and Seesen pilot

**Decision.** Earn trust by publishing the design decision a generic solar buying guide cannot make for a specific site. Start with canopy geometry and when an EV is present. The visible output is a project brief a homeowner and installer can both use.

## The asset that compounds

Use one project record across the editorial, sales and research work. Start with a drawing that identifies the module version, roof footprint, intended function, drainage route and planned load. Give every commercial example a date and source. Attach the admitted assumptions to the public example. A buyer should be able to carry the brief to any installer.

| Audience | Decision | First useful output | Evidence earned next |
|---|---|---|---|
| German project owner | Will the canopy fit and what scope am I buying? | German brief and itemized quote comparison | Dimensions reviewed and next step agreed |
| Installer or planner | Which module, roof grid and package can I quote? | Comparable bill of materials and open responsibilities | Revised layout, engineering owner and excluded work |
| Researcher | Which claim can this site actually test? | Protocol, comparison design and field log | Paired observations with coverage and uncertainty |
| FrankX reader | Where do advances in modules matter? | Three real module silhouettes and the 24-hour parking comparison | A project question that can be observed and measured |

The remote preview implements the page, interactive displays, public-source links, template downloads and outbound click events. Alexander's team still needs to validate each commercial claim and own the quote and contract. Store any buyer details in the organization handling the inquiry. A template download carries no personal data into the seller's workflow.

## Offers and economics

1. **Kit.** Publish an annotated footprint and the exact scope. Show modules, structure, drainage, equipment, collection/delivery and exclusions. Keep a written revision and effective date.
2. **Kit with energy equipment.** Add storage and charging against a documented use pattern. Offer transparent deltas for wallbox, battery and any electrician scope. Show what changes at the site when a buyer selects each option.
3. **Designed and installed.** Quote an agreed project boundary with responsibilities for site survey, engineering, groundwork, installation, commissioning and support. Test a paid planning credit only after confirming who produces the drawings, how long it takes and how the credit applies.

Margin model per qualified inquiry = probability of win × (net revenue less direct install, freight, equipment, survey and expected callback cost) less presale handling cost. Compare this with kit-only contribution. A more expensive offer earns its place when it resolves work a buyer would otherwise coordinate and still increases contribution. Record the commercial owner and tax basis before showing a public price.

## First 30 days

- **Week one.** Agree with Alexander on two offers, who owns each scope, the price date and what can be shown. Get permission for one current installed project and request the same four shots every time: approach, underside, drainage exit, electrical boundary. Use the older public supplier video for the construction discussion until the current footage is approved.
- **Week two.** Use the existing German offer path. Publish one English FrankX research note with the fit experiment and one German LinkedIn piece about the extra module row. Give both links tagged campaign URLs. Add the German project brief to seller replies once Alexander approves the workflow.
- **Week three.** Review the first eligible inquiries together. Record which dimension or missing scope blocked pricing, whether the buyer wanted a kit or installation, and actual presales effort. Rewrite the brief around the missing evidence that occurs repeatedly.
- **Week four.** Compare qualified inquiries, quotes, close rate and gross contribution across kit and extended scope. Inspect reasons for losses. Decide whether workplace charging deserves a second page. Keep a separate pipeline for sample-site research discussions.

Suggested owner view: source and cohort date, template, offer selected, lead qualification reason, quote amount, gross contribution estimate, outcome, elapsed time, and scope gaps. Site events count template link clicks and outbound offer opens. A lead, quote or sale exists only after the seller records it. Review sample size and missing outcomes before drawing channel conclusions. Avoid collecting photos or inquiry details on the editorial page.

## The proposed research pilot

Register one primary hypothesis, an outcome, and eligibility rules before collection. Recruit a comparison with controllable rear exposure and document module, wiring and reference equivalence. Where matching is impossible, publish the time series and associations with narrower claims. Invite academic input on the protocol as a proposal. A university affiliation or commitment requires explicit agreement from the institution.

Distribute the first measured memo with the site drawing, pre-specified outcome, exclusions, paired-period chart, data dictionary and releasable aggregate data. One good measured project can improve the next quote, give installers a useful reference and give the editorial work a reason to exist.
