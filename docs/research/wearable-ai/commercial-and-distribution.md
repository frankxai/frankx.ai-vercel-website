## Paid product specification

Working name: **Starlight Wearable AI Developer Kit**. Proposed extension of existing Starlight packages, not a claim of an existing wearable product.

Buyer: agencies and indie teams building human-reviewed camera-to-record workflows across two device families. Job: reduce time lost to changing SDKs, compatibility uncertainty and unsafe action handoffs.

### Deliverables before sale

- Two maintained adapters, each with exact tested SDK/device/firmware and an unsupported-feature contract.
- Recorded native install/run evidence and hardware validation checklist.
- Source fixtures and a capability-negotiation contract; denied permissions and disconnect recovery.
- Multimodal gateway integration against the existing Starlight identity/memory contracts after tenant review.
- Proposal/confirmation/idempotency integration with one approved business destination.
- Cost meter, deletion/export workflow, troubleshooting, updates and documented deprecation policy.
- Example applications for documentation and creator shot logs; no safety-critical claims.
