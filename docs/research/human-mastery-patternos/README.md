# Human Mastery PatternOS v0

Status: **draft canonical substrate**  
Version: `0.1.0`  
Canonical namespace: `HMP`  
Updated: 2026-08-16

## Executive decision

Build one evidence-aware capability graph, not a collection of disconnected self-improvement pages. The launch wedge is:

> **Pattern recognition → creative problem solving → human–AI facilitation**

The public research hub, library, books, guides, workshops, assessments, infographics, and future agent interfaces are projections of the same typed source-to-outcome chain:

`Source → Atomic claim → Synthesis → Pattern → Protocol → Drill or assessment → Observation → Outcome → Revision`

The durable advantage is not a large reading list. It is the accumulated transfer graph: which pattern, in which context, applied through which intervention, produced which observable outcome, under which boundary conditions.

## Constitution

1. **Operational units beat trait labels.** Model a capability as `capability × context × observable behavior × criterion × boundary condition`; never as a flattering identity claim.
2. **Evidence and experience remain separate.** Every publishable object carries an E/F/T/R passport. Scientific confidence, field validation, transfer validation, and rights are not averaged into one score.
3. **Practitioner media generates hypotheses.** Tony Robbins, educators, speakers, creators, and YouTubers can supply useful language and drills; they are not research anchors unless the underlying claim is independently sourced.
4. **Transfer is the moat.** A pattern becomes valuable when it survives measurement across contexts, not when it sounds universal.
5. **Humans retain consequential authority.** AI may propose, compare, simulate, summarize, and monitor. Humans own values, consent, irreversible decisions, exceptions, and accountability.
6. **Prediction precedes feedback.** Capture the expected result before testing so learning is not rewritten after the fact.
7. **Generation and judgment separate.** Divergence, criticism, and final judgment use explicit roles and timeboxes to prevent premature convergence and agreeable model collapse.
8. **Failures become assets.** Every serious failure records the signal, violated invariant, recovery move, and revision to the pattern or protocol.
9. **Rights travel with the object.** A link or official embed does not grant permission to reproduce a transcript, illustration, exercise, or paid text.
10. **No object is finished.** Version, review date, falsifiers, and next validation remain visible. Deprecation is a normal knowledge outcome.

## Five capability planes

| Plane | Scope | Representative outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Regulate & learn | attention, emotion regulation, motivation, deliberate learning, recovery | sustained practice, calibrated confidence, faster correction |
| Relate & express | listening, charisma, communication, speaking, facilitation | accurate understanding, credible expression, useful group dialogue |
| Sense, decide & create | observation, analogy, judgment, forecasting, creativity, problem solving | better hypotheses, options, decisions, and artifacts |
| Lead & coordinate | psychological safety, shared models, delegation, leadership, team learning | coordinated action, speak-up behavior, resilient execution |
| Design, market & operate | design, web, experimentation, marketing, sales, funnels, flow | useful experiences, causal learning, customer fit, reliable operations |

These planes are lenses, not silos. A single pattern may operate across several planes.

## Pattern Intelligence loop

1. Observe
2. Encode
3. Compare
4. Hypothesize
5. Generate
6. Test
7. Operationalize
8. Debrief

This loop is a working synthesis, not a claimed law of cognition. Each stage must be tied to an observable artifact or decision.

IDs are stable. Titles and wording may change without changing identity. Breaking semantic changes require a new ID and a supersession link.

## Commercial boundary

Foundational knowledge, citations, limitations, and basic patterns should remain publicly inspectable. Paid value may come from calibrated transfer: guided practice, team protocols, facilitation, benchmarking, private outcome instrumentation, organization-specific mappings, and runtime support.

## Publication gate

An object may be published only when its referenced sources resolve, its E/F/T/R passport is complete, its claim language matches its evidence level, its rights scope allows the proposed use, its failure modes and boundary conditions are visible, and a next validation has been named. Public interface implementation remains blocked until one of the three experience directions is selected and mobile evidence is captured.
