# Five laws for /research

Author: opencode/designer subagent. Date: 2026-11-08. Session: local.

Compressed from `design.md` (sections 1, 3), `taste.md` (North Star, restraint test, density, frozen decisions, polish pass), `VISUAL-SYSTEM.md` (principles 1 to 7, research placement) and the `/research` brief in `PAGE-BRIEFS.md`. No new philosophy; each law is a test that can fail.

### 1. A number names its file

The Architect duty earns trust with proof, and page briefs require every number to have a source file and an as-of date. `VISUAL-SYSTEM.md` section 2 says a data figure cites source and date in the figure itself.

**Fail test:** pick any figure on the page and try to reach its file or its date in one line. If the answer is "the designer typed it", the page fails.

### 2. One primary action, repeated at most

`taste.md` calls two equal CTAs design indecision. The brief names one action: read the investigations.

**Fail test:** count the buttons of equal weight. If they lead to more than one destination, it fails. The same action repeated at the end of the scroll still counts as one.

### 3. Every block names its job or leaves

The restraint test in `taste.md` is five questions long and all five reduce to one: does this block have a job you can state in a sentence.

**Fail test:** delete the block and read the page. If nothing is harder to understand, the block failed and should stay deleted.

### 4. Evidence keeps its labels at the point of use

`VISUAL-SYSTEM.md` requires source, date and n on every research figure, and the publication boundary keeps unreviewed material off the hub. A grade without its definition is decoration.

**Fail test:** check each grade, count and dataset for its label and date in the same block. An unknown renders `[?]`; a guess fails.

### 5. The phone, the keyboard and reduced motion are the same page

`taste.md` says mobile is a different rhythm, and the polish pass requires a visible focus ring at every stop and a real page under reduced motion.

**Fail test:** at 375 the headline and the primary action must sit in the first screen; every control must be reachable by Tab with a visible ring; nothing may animate when the reader has asked for no motion.
