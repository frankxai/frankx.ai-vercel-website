# Connect authority direction decision

Date: 2026-08-24  
Surface: `/connect`, with relationship continuity on `/partnerships`  
Decision status: Direction 01 selected for the draft implementation; production approval remains attached to the release gate.

## Release thesis

- Recipient: an established operator, founder, technical leader, or potential partner deciding whether Frank's judgment is worth trusting.
- Prior state: a synthetic-avatar link hub with equal-weight role cards, stale event language, and proof separated from the claims it was meant to support.
- Observed truth: Frank has owned documentary workshop and presentation photography, a public 99-agent catalog, a source-led architecture field guide, and a 12,000-plus-experiment music practice.
- Proposition: Frank turns complex AI decisions into systems people can operate.
- Consequence: a qualified visitor can inspect the work, choose a situation-specific path, and start one direct human conversation.

## Exactly three directions

### 01 · Working intelligence — selected

Warm paper, deep ink, one cobalt decision mark, a decisive documentary crop, asymmetric editorial composition, and proof expressed as margin notes and evidence rows. The still frame carries the full meaning; no authored entrance sequence is required.

Why selected:

- Makes Frank, the work, and the next action legible in two seconds.
- Matches the active FrankX foundation territory for editorial and relationship surfaces.
- Feels human and expert without borrowing authority from a company logo wall.
- Extends naturally from `/connect` into `/partnerships` while allowing technical routes to retain their own product mode.

Artifact: `direction-01-working-intelligence.png`

### 02 · Engineer's atelier

Aluminum, paper, oxide, a workshop crop, utilitarian type, and an exploded decision flow. One possible authored motion would assemble question → boundary → evidence → handoff.

Why not selected:

- Strong mechanism story, but risks reading as industrial cosplay before the new identity system is fully settled.
- Puts more visual weight on apparatus than on Frank's judgment and relationship with the visitor.

Artifact: `direction-02-engineers-atelier.png`

### 03 · Field dossier

Stark black and white, editorial serif contrast, documentary photography, evidence-first language, and no color or motion.

Why not selected:

- Strongest evidence posture, but the first impression is more institutional and distant than the Connect job requires.
- Better suited to a research report or case-study system than a relationship entry point.

Artifact: `direction-03-field-dossier.png`

## Motion decision

Authored motion is cut for this release. The flagship still frame is complete without it, the active identity reset prioritizes composition before animation, and the core job is trust and orientation. Only restrained hover/focus feedback remains, with reduced-motion-safe CSS transitions.

## Typography decision

The draft uses the existing Inter family already loaded by the site. No unapproved commercial or trial font is introduced during the identity reset. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, line length, and spacing rather than a new font claim.

## Image and disclosure decision

Only owned documentary images already present in the repository are used. Captions disclose that the photographs come from Frank's former Oracle role and explicitly state that no current endorsement is implied.
