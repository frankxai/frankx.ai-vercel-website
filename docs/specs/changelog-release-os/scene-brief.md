# Changelog Scene Brief

## Scene

A quiet release room after a meaningful deployment: the headline is large and editorial; the proof is precise, compact, and inspectable. The page should feel maintained, not marketed.

## Hierarchy

- **Primary:** latest release title and outcome.
- **Secondary:** chronological release ledger.
- **Tertiary:** proof receipts, categories, dates, RSS, and repository links.

## Material and rhythm

- Void background with hairline white borders.
- One restrained emerald signal line for current work; cyan is reserved for verification links.
- Large areas of negative space and a narrow reading column on detail pages.
- Mono labels for dates, categories, and proof counters.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: release date rail and editorial content form an asymmetric two-column ledger.
- Mobile: date rail collapses above content; all proof links remain full-width tap targets.
- No meaning depends on hover or color.

## Explicit exclusions

- No generated hero image, orbital motif, glass card wall, gradient headline, fake terminal, animated counters, or decorative metric grid.
- No raw commit hashes or lines-of-code statistics on the public surface.
