## Three directions considered

1. **Editorial field guide.** Asymmetric introduction and working-record specimen;
   strong display heading, restrained mono labels, linked editorial rows and a
   static workflow. No new illustrative imagery or autonomous motion. Best fit
   for a reader choosing a method and inspecting sources. Selected.
2. **Operator workbench.** Compact interface typography, equal data/input panes,
   a comparison table as the main visual and input-driven state changes. Better
   for repeated account-data work after authenticated reports are available.
   Rejected for this release: no verified account data to populate it.
3. **Learning path.** Sequential lessons with progress navigation, longer text
   specimens, documentary video as the visual anchor and explicit completion
   transitions. Better for an established course. Rejected for this release:
   completed walkthrough footage and tested learner progression do not exist.
