# FrankX Founder Language Code

This is the canonical messaging adapter for public FrankX pages, offers, newsletters, and agent-generated drafts.

## One avatar

Speak to **the founder**.

Entrepreneur, co-founder, solopreneur, coach, expert, and creator-led operator are contexts—not separate avatars—when the person carries risk, makes consequential decisions, and owns the outcome.

This is a language and routing adapter, not an erasure instruction. FrankX remains a living studio and portfolio: AI architecture, founder systems, music, books, experiments, research, and the Human Layer reinforce one another. Apply the founder lens by naming a consequential situation and offering the right next route—not by rewriting every surface into generic founder coaching or replacing the homepage. The homepage is governed by [`HOMEPAGE-PRESERVATION-CONTRACT.md`](./HOMEPAGE-PRESERVATION-CONTRACT.md).

Preferred second person:

- “You are building…”
- “Your current constraint…”
- “The decision only you can make…”

Avoid demographic roll calls such as “founders, creators, coaches, consultants, entrepreneurs, and solopreneurs.” Name the founder once, then describe the situation precisely.

## The Founder Stack

FrankX organizes founder work through five operating layers:

1. **State** — attention, energy, emotional range, and decision capacity.
2. **Signal** — positioning, customer truth, founder voice, and earned beliefs.
3. **Systems** — delivery, AI architecture, memory, operating rhythm, and quality controls.
4. **Scale** — offers, distribution, reusable IP, and leverage.
5. **Stewardship** — judgment, boundaries, ownership, relationships, and durable standards.

Use “five layers, one current constraint.” Do not imply that one score diagnoses a person or guarantees a business result.

## The three moves

- **Diagnose** — map the constraint with the Founder Stack or the specialist Founder Signal scan.
- **Install** — build the operating capability through the Foundry.
- **Compound** — improve consequential bets through Founder’s Circle and continue learning through the Signal Loop.

The primary public route is:

`Founder Stack → useful next move → Signal Loop`

The Signal Loop remains an umbrella newsletter. Specialist streams remain visible and opt-in; do not imply that a preference was saved until the backend confirms it.

## Human Layer: post-rational, not anti-rational

FrankX can hold empirical evidence, direct experience, and symbolic meaning without pretending they are interchangeable. Every Human Layer claim uses exactly one lens:

- **Established** — supported by converging evidence and credible consensus. State the population and boundary when relevant.
- **Emerging** — plausible and actively studied, but not settled. Use “may,” “early evidence,” and named uncertainty.
- **Experiential** — a report, practice, or first-person observation. Never universalize it.
- **Symbolic** — metaphor, spiritual frame, ritual, or meaning-making language. Never present it as a laboratory mechanism.

“Post-rational” means reason remains available while experience and meaning are also allowed. It does not mean science proves spiritual cosmology.

## Practice vocabulary

- **Meditation:** attention training, awareness practice, contemplative practice.
- **Breathwork:** distinguish studied slow breathing from intensive practices; add contraindications where relevant.
- **Sound:** deep listening, entrainment research, musical ritual, or subjective regulation. Never claim a frequency cures or deterministically creates a state.
- **Neurotechnology:** name the device, measurement, evidence level, and limits; do not use “neuro” as decorative authority.
- **Mind-body recovery:** sleep, stress regulation, movement, interoception, and recovery practices; not medical treatment.
- **Dream practice:** reflection, imagery, memory, and meaning-making; metaphysical interpretation is symbolic.
- **Manifestation:** attention, mental rehearsal, expectancy, behavior, and aligned action. Cosmological causation is symbolic unless a claim is explicitly being examined and rejected.
- **Silva Method:** describe as a branded guided-imagery and mental-rehearsal system; separate historical claims from supported mechanisms.
- **Plant medicine:** research, legal context, risk, integration ethics, and harm reduction only. No sourcing, dosing, protocols, contraindication overrides, or treatment promises.

## Voice register

Use:

- grounded authority
- inspectable proof
- specific nouns and verbs
- uncertainty when uncertainty is real
- “useful,” “bounded,” “current constraint,” “founder judgment,” “operating layer,” “from the source”

Avoid:

- guru titles or follower language
- “quantum” as a prestige word
- “healing frequency,” “cellular reprogramming,” or “science proves manifestation”
- “unlock,” “unleash,” “revolutionary,” “Godmode,” “sovereign” as unearned product superlatives
- universal outcomes, fake scarcity, unverified counts, and unsupported time-to-result claims

## Claims and proof

For every commercial or Human Layer claim, be able to answer:

1. What exactly is being claimed?
2. Which lens applies?
3. What source, implementation receipt, or first-person boundary supports it?
4. What could a reasonable reader incorrectly infer?
5. Is the claim still current?

If the proof is not available, soften or remove the claim. Never use a future implementation as present-tense evidence.

## Register boundaries

- **FrankX:** founder operating systems, AI architecture, Human Layer, studio work, direct professional language.
- **Arcanea:** mythology, Guardians, Realms, Gates, and fantasy cosmology.
- **SIS/ACOS:** system substrate and agent implementation terms when they materially help the founder understand the work.

Do not leak Arcanea mythology into FrankX conversion copy. Human Layer symbolic language remains clearly labeled and does not become brand cosmology.
