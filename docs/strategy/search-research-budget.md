# Search research budget and evidence policy

Applies to existing Monday intelligence, SEO review and SEO specialist workflows. This is a prompt-level contract, not a provider-enforced quota or a new monitoring service.

## Before a metered request

1. Name the page decision, audience, market and owning URL. Inspect repository inventory and saved evidence first. Use first-party Search Console/Bing exports for own-site performance.
2. Read current entitlement, balance, reset date and report tariff through an authorized account surface. Unknown access, balance, cost or allowance means **zero paid execution**. Connector presence does not prove entitlement.
3. Discover the report and retrieve its live schema. Calculate worst-case units using the documented tariff and requested rows or inputs. Token count and field count do not establish the tariff.
4. Record owner-approved allowance, reservations and consumption in the existing private research evidence record. One aggregate allowance covers every agent/client. Reserve before execution; reconcile against provider balance afterward. When account-wide reconciliation is unavailable, stop concurrent paid work.
5. Proposed sprint ceiling: `min(5,000 units, 50% of verified remaining units)`; proposed recurring ceiling: 2,000 units/week inside the same authorized sprint/month envelope. These ceilings grant no spending authorization. No automatic top-up, plan change or overage.

## Bound requests

- Default 25 rows, hard ceiling 50 per request. Set an explicit schema-supported limit. When absent, prove a finite input/output bound or do not execute.
- Batch summaries: at most 40 phrases and one database. The exposed `phrase_these` schema bounds work through semicolon-separated inputs, not `display_limit`.
- One site and at most two task-relevant competitors. US is the English research default; UK/NL need a specific audience decision. Do not pool countries.
- Question/related expansion: at most two selected seeds, 20 rows each. No recursive expansion, all-country runs, implicit pagination or historical grids.
- Domain keyword/page reports: at most 50 rows per target. Partial/empty results do not establish a complete baseline.
- Historical, backlink, traffic/audience and project reports need their own entitlement and tariff checks. SEO access does not imply Trends API or every project tool.

## Reuse evidence

Deduplicate by provider, report, normalized/sorted parameters, database, device and data period. Read an existing receipt before executing. Keep raw vendor results and billing/account records private.

Refresh eligibility: keyword summaries 30 days; competitor snapshots 14 days; volatile SERP checks 7 days. Expiry invites review; it does not authorize another request. Retain dated historical evidence.

Dashboards, CMS previews, article drafting, linking and repeated reasoning read saved evidence. They must not trigger live research on every load. A renamed task or different client does not reset the budget.

## Failures

- No automatic retry of quota, authorization, unknown-cost or empty responses. Save the failure and use existing evidence.
- At most one deliberate transient-error retry after reserving its worst-case cost inside the same allowance. Never assume failure was free without provider confirmation.
- Stop when predicted charge exceeds allowance, balance is stale, entitlement is unclear, or concurrency prevents reconciliation.
- Never invent rankings, traffic, volume, difficulty, citation rates or conversions. Unknown is not zero. Synthetic fixtures cannot enter performance reports.

## Evidence and CMS handoff

Use the existing evidence template: report, exact parameters without secrets, database, language/device when known, retrieval time, data period, coverage, private raw snapshot, source type, expected/actual units and reviewer.

The brief maps a query family to one owning URL, existing coverage, user job, sources/unknowns, useful next action and acceptance. Refresh before duplicating pages. Keep public content in the existing MDX/typed data system and branch/PR workflow. Research cannot directly publish or migrate the CMS.

Technical crawls, headings, link inventories and page extraction can use existing local tooling or bounded Firecrawl/Apify after checking that provider's budget. Crawling cannot recreate a proprietary keyword-volume, difficulty or backlink index. Use one extraction provider per task.

## Acceptance

End with a page decision or insufficient-evidence result, saved receipts, aggregate budget reconciliation and next review action. Keep search estimates, answer-tool observations, referrals, opt-ins and paid activation distinct. Do not impose entity-density targets or manufacture statistics to satisfy a formula.
