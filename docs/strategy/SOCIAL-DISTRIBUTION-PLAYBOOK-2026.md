## 3. Brand Voice & Quality Control

Every piece of social copy must strictly adhere to the FrankX Brand Standard:
- **Title:** "AI Architect" (never "AI Systems Architect" or "Senior AI Architect").
- **Voice:** "Elite Creator. AI Architect. Humble Excellence."
- **Strict Banned Cliché List:** `delve`, `dive deep`, `game-changing`, `revolutionary`, `unlock the power of`, `unleash`, `it's worth noting`.
- **Evidence-First:** Every claim must be accompanied by a benchmark percentage, mathematical formula, or architecture diagram.
