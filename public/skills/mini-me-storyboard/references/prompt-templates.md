# Prompt templates

Use placeholders, then replace them with the person's actual reference traits and the approved scene brief.

## Character bible

```text
Inspect the approved identity photos. Separate stable facial traits from lighting, age cues, camera angle, clothing, and any prior AI art style. Produce a compact character bible: face and hair, beard distribution, eye shape, body proportions, default and alternate outfits, eyewear rule, expressions, and 'do not change' traits. Treat other pictured people separately. Identify any conflicts that affect likeness.
```

## Character sheet

```text
Create one refined stylized 3D character sheet from the identity photos and approved bible. Show front, three-quarter and side views plus two full-body actions. Human proportions, expressive facial geometry, tactile materials, soft cinematic light. Preserve [DISTINCTIVE TRAITS] and show [DEFAULT] / [ALTERNATE OUTFIT]. No invented logos or extra people. This sheet becomes the continuity reference.
```

## Ten-card storyboard

```text
Plan ten [4:5 / 9:16] posts for [AUDIENCE] about [THEME]. Each needs a six-word title, one insight, emotional beat, everyday setting, physical miniature action, verified role of any companion, camera/light, source or proof, caption idea, and alt text. Use at least six distinct visual grammars. Build toward a human invitation. Label official, inspired, and original characters. Do not generate images yet.
```

## One scene

```text
Create ONE full-bleed [ASPECT] image, no collage or contact sheet. Reference [X] controls identity; reference [Y] controls the location; reference [Z] is style only. Mini [PERSON] [ACTION] on [SURFACE] while [COMPANION] [COUNTER-ACTION]. The visual turning point is [CHANGE]. Preserve [PHOTO REGIONS] exactly if editing. Match contact shadow, perspective, scale, reflected light and depth of field. One focal point, legible negative space and phone-safe composition. No invented text.
```

## Infographic plate

```text
Illustrate [ONE PROCESS] as a [ASPECT] scene with three physical actions and reserved clean areas for a headline and three short labels. Do not render factual copy. Supply exact copy, reading order, alt text, and source notes separately. Overlay accurate typography and arrows in deterministic layout. Check central safe area at phone size.
```

## Revision

```text
Compare the image with the approved identity and scene brief. Score likeness, action, physical contact, crop, readability, brand provenance, and emotional clarity from 1-5. Point to the three largest defects and revise those regions only. Preserve what already works. Export with prompt, references, caption, alt text, provenance note, dimensions, and change log.
```
