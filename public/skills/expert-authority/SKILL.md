# Expert Authority Intelligence

Use this skill when an expert, creator, educator, consultant, founder, or author needs to convert scattered expertise into a coherent authority business.

The commercial product is one **Expert Authority Intelligence System**. It contains five governed engines:

1. **Expert Intelligence / Canon Engine**
2. **Audience Intelligence / Signal Engine**
3. **Authority Engine / Synthesis Engine**
4. **Product Intelligence / Offer Engine**
5. **Funnel Intelligence / Growth Engine**

Do not present these as five unrelated products. Each engine produces an artifact consumed by the next engine. Evidence from delivery and conversion returns to the system.

## Required inputs

Collect the strongest available evidence. Mark missing inputs rather than inventing them.

### Expert evidence

- biography and career chronology
- books, articles, talks, podcasts, transcripts, notes, and courses
- recurring decisions and diagnostic questions
- named and unnamed frameworks
- client outcomes, testimonials, metrics, and proof
- stories, failures, turning points, and contrarian beliefs
- existing offers, prices, delivery models, and constraints

### Audience evidence

- discovery and sales-call transcripts
- comments, direct messages, community questions, and support tickets
- survey and quiz responses
- webinar questions and chat logs
- search queries and content performance
- objections, churn reasons, refunds, and non-purchases
- exact phrases used to describe pain, desired outcomes, and alternatives

### Commercial evidence

- traffic by source
- lead-capture and conversion rates
- webinar, challenge, or quiz completion
- email engagement and reply themes
- purchase, completion, ascension, retention, and referral
- delivery effort, gross margin, time-to-value, and proof generated

## Non-negotiable operating rules

1. **Evidence precedes positioning.** Do not generate a niche or category from biography alone.
2. **Commercial value is not mention frequency.** Rank problems by urgency, consequence, access, and willingness to pay.
3. **One mechanism governs the ladder.** Free, entry, core, and premium offers must resolve successive buyer constraints.
4. **Traffic does not fragment the funnel.** LinkedIn, YouTube, and Instagram route into one diagnostic and segmented conversion spine.
5. **Claims require proof.** Distinguish demonstrated outcomes, plausible hypotheses, and unsupported aspirations.
6. **Human judgment remains authoritative.** Agents propose, cite, compare, and test. The operator approves positioning, promises, pricing, and publication.
7. **Every cycle updates the system.** Outcomes, objections, conversions, completion, and failures become new evidence.

## Workflow

### Phase 0 — Evidence ledger

Create an evidence ledger before synthesis.

For every source record:

- source ID
- source type
- date
- audience or client segment
- provenance URL or file path
- direct evidence
- interpretation
- confidence: high, medium, low
- contradiction or missing context
- permission and privacy constraints

Return:

- source inventory
- evidence gaps
- contradictions requiring operator resolution
- claims that must not be made yet

### Phase 1 — Canon Engine

Extract the expert canon into the following objects:

- **Principles:** stable beliefs that govern decisions
- **Decisions:** recurring judgment patterns and trade-offs
- **Methods:** explicit and implicit processes
- **Mechanisms:** why the method should create the outcome
- **Diagnostics:** questions used to classify a situation
- **Stories:** moments that encode identity, tension, and proof
- **Proof:** outcomes, credentials, artifacts, demonstrations, and external validation
- **Language:** phrases that should remain verbatim
- **Boundaries:** what the expert does not claim, serve, or recommend

Required artifact: `expert-canon.md` or equivalent structured graph.

Quality gate:

- Every major claim cites evidence.
- Contradictions remain visible.
- The canon distinguishes operator belief from market proof.
- An agent can answer a domain question using the canon without flattening the expert voice.

### Phase 2 — Signal Engine

Cluster audience evidence by:

- desired outcome
- triggering event
- current workaround
- perceived obstacle
- emotional and economic consequence
- objection
- urgency
- awareness level
- willingness to pay
- exact language
- segment
- evidence count

Then rank opportunities using:

`Opportunity score = urgency × consequence × access × proof-fit × willingness-to-pay`

Use a 1–5 scale for each term. Keep raw counts separate from the opportunity score.

Required artifact: `audience-signal-graph.md` or equivalent database.

Quality gate:

- Representative phrases remain verbatim.
- High-frequency low-value complaints do not outrank costly urgent problems.
- Every segment includes disconfirming evidence.
- The system can explain why one problem was selected over another.

### Phase 3 — Authority Engine

Generate no more than three candidate authority positions. Compare them, then select one.

Each candidate must define:

- ideal buyer and triggering event
- costly problem
- rejected alternatives
- contrarian or differentiated point of view
- named mechanism
- proof stack
- category language
- content pillars
- claims permitted now
- proof required for stronger claims
- strategic exclusions

Score candidates on:

- market urgency
- expert credibility
- proof density
- recognizability
- defensibility
- product depth
- distribution fit

Required artifact: `authority-position.md`.

Quality gate:

A competent stranger can complete this sentence without ambiguity:

> This is the obvious expert for ______ when ______ because ______.

### Phase 4 — Offer Engine

Construct one product stepladder around the selected mechanism.

| Tier | Buyer state | Purpose | Typical price |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Diagnose | Unclear constraint | Personalized map and next move | Free |
| Activate | Understands problem, lacks momentum | Visible first result | €27–€97 |
| Systemize | Has expertise, lacks commercial architecture | Canon, audience graph, ladder, funnel | €197–€497 |
| Transform | Wants implementation with accountability | Complete authority business | €1,500–€5,000 |
| Implement | Requires custom infrastructure | Intelligence graph, agents, workflows, governance | €15,000–€100,000+ |

For every tier specify:

- buyer state
- promised transition
- mechanism
- inputs
- deliverables
- time-to-value
- completion event
- evidence generated
- delivery model
- price logic
- gross-margin assumptions
- next purchase
- non-fit criteria

Required artifacts:

- `product-ladder.md`
- `course-architecture.md` for educational products
- `offer-evidence-plan.md`

Quality gate:

- Each product changes a buyer state.
- The curriculum follows transformation sequence, not topic inventory.
- Cross-sells solve the next constraint rather than adding unrelated content.
- Price reflects consequence, implementation depth, evidence, and delivery cost.

### Phase 5 — Growth Engine

Assign each channel one dominant job:

- **LinkedIn:** category argument, proof, operator judgment
- **YouTube:** depth, demonstrations, transformations, trust
- **Instagram:** identity, reach, emotional recognition, social proof

Route all channels into one diagnostic. Segment follow-up by the weakest engine or buyer state.

Standard conversion spine:

`Content → Diagnostic → Personalized Result → Free Skill or Agent → Challenge / Webinar → Entry Product → Core Transformation → Implementation → Evidence Return`

For every funnel stage define:

- entry source
- segment
- promise
- artifact or experience
- primary metric
- drop-off threshold
- follow-up logic
- next best action
- experiment owner
- stop condition
- evidence written back to the system

Required artifacts:

- `traffic-to-revenue-map.md`
- `email-sequences.md`
- `experiment-backlog.md`
- `measurement-contract.md`

Quality gate:

- Attribution survives across channels.
- Email sequences differ by segment and stage.
- Webinar, challenge, and quiz are selected according to buyer state rather than fashion.
- Every experiment has a hypothesis and stop condition.

## Course-construction contract

When building a course, start with the target capability and observable completion event.

For each module define:

1. buyer state before the module
2. decision or capability gained
3. minimal concept required
4. action performed
5. artifact produced
6. feedback mechanism
7. proof of completion
8. prerequisite for the next module

Reject modules that only transfer information without changing capability or producing evidence.

## Agent team

The skill can run as one agent or a governed five-agent team.

### Canon Curator

Owns provenance, expert voice, methods, proof, contradictions, and boundaries.

Output contract:

- canonical objects
- citations
- confidence
- contradictions
- missing evidence

### Audience Analyst

Owns audience language, segmentation, demand ranking, objections, and commercial evidence.

Output contract:

- clusters
- evidence counts
- representative language
- opportunity scores
- disconfirming signals

### Authority Strategist

Owns category position, point of view, mechanism, proof stack, and exclusions.

Output contract:

- candidate positions
- comparative scoring
- selected position
- unsupported claims

### Offer Architect

Owns transformation sequence, curriculum, product ladder, price logic, and evidence plan.

Output contract:

- tier definitions
- buyer-state transitions
- delivery architecture
- price rationale
- next-purchase logic

### Growth Architect

Owns traffic roles, diagnostic segmentation, conversion events, email, cross-sell, attribution, and experiments.

Output contract:

- channel map
- funnel logic
- sequences
- metrics
- experiment backlog

### Human Authority Gate

The operator approves:

- selected position
- named mechanism
- public claims
- offer promise
- price
- publication
- automated outbound communication
