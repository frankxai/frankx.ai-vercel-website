# ADR-0001: Every answer cites a clause, or the system refuses

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The failure that started this project was someone quoting a notice period from a superseded
amendment (`01-discovery.md` § 11). The expensive property of that failure is not that the answer
was wrong; it is that nothing about it looked wrong. A fluent paragraph about a contract reads the
same whether it came from the clause in force, a clause from 2023, or nothing at all.

Nobody reviews each answer (`01-discovery.md` § 32). Sixty people self-serve and read the output
alone. Whatever check exists has to be inside the answer itself.

## Decision

Every claim in an answer carries a clause id, and the clause id resolves to text the asker can open
in the contract management system. When retrieval returns nothing in force on the question's date,
the system refuses and names the contract owner. When the question is a request for advice or is
about a contract in negotiation, the system refuses and names a lawyer. A hedged paraphrase is not
an allowed output: the choice is a cited answer or a named person.

## Consequences

Easier: an answer is checkable in one click, the weekly citation audit has something mechanical to
sample, and the eval suite can grade citations rather than prose.

Harder: the system refuses more often than a system that is willing to summarise. That is the
intended trade — a refusal costs the asker a message to a lawyer, and a plausible uncited answer
costs the firm a contractual position.

Open risk: citation is not comprehension. An asker who does not open the citation has no check at
all, and nothing in the system can make them. The weekly audit of 40 answers is the only systemic
compensation, and it samples rather than covers.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| Answer with a confidence score instead of citations | A number the system assigns to itself, checkable by nobody |
| Answer freely, with a disclaimer | The disclaimer is read once and the answers are read daily |
| Return clauses only, no prose answer | This is the existing full-text search, which finds documents and leaves the twenty minutes intact (`01-discovery.md` § 3) |

## Trust boundary

Answers are T3, because the model read T3 clause text to produce them. They are shown to an asker
and never fed back into a later prompt. Refusals name people, not systems, which keeps escalation
outside the machine.
