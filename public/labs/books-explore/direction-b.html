Keep room for another reading.

18 pixel reading text

A useful reading room gives a book enough space to be itself. The page keeps a comfortable line length. The reader can enlarge the text, follow a chapter and return to the shelf without losing their bearings.

A technical chapter may need a diagram, a source note or a working example. A poem may need a quiet space after its last line. Those differences should guide the edition. The interface does its work when the reader can attend to the words.
