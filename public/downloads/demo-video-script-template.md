# Demo video script template

From the FrankX guide on recording demos: https://frankx.ai/guides/product-demo-video-guide

Use this before you press record. Planning on one page lets you record and re-record each part on its own, instead of restarting the whole video.

---

Length targets that work in practice:

- Sales follow-up or personal reply: 60 to 120 seconds
- Landing page or launch demo: 60 to 90 seconds
- Investor or team update: 2 to 4 minutes
- Onboarding walkthrough: 2 to 5 minutes per task
- Course lesson or tutorial: 5 to 12 minutes per lesson

## 2. The structure

Write what you will say in plain sentences. Keep each block short enough to re-record on its own.

### Block 1: The problem in their words (0 to 10 seconds)

Say the problem the way the viewer would say it. No company history, no "hi, I'm".

> Example: "If your team still writes the weekly report by hand, this is the 90 seconds that removes it."

### Block 2: Where we are (10 to 20 seconds)

One sentence of context so the screen makes sense.

> Example: "This is the dashboard your account manager sees on Monday morning."

### Block 3: Show the job getting done (20 to 70 seconds)

One workflow, real data, start to finish. Cut every click that does not move the job forward.

### Block 4: The proof moment (a few seconds)

Stop and let it land. Zoom in on the result. Say what changed in one sentence.

### Block 5: One next step (last 5 to 10 seconds)

One action. A link, a reply, a calendar slot. Not three.

## 3. Shot list

Plan each clip. Recording in short clips means a mistake costs you one clip, not the whole take.

| # | On screen | What you say | Layout | Seconds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | Camera large | |
| 2 | | | Screen + camera bubble | |
| 3 | | | Screen only, zoom on result | |
| 4 | | | Camera large | |

Layout guide:

- **Camera large** for the opening and the ask. Faces build trust.
- **Screen with camera bubble** while you explain.
- **Screen only** when the detail matters more than you do.

---

## 4. Recording checklist

- [ ] Notifications off (system, Slack, mail, calendar)
- [ ] Clean browser profile, no personal bookmarks or tabs
- [ ] Demo account with realistic data, no customer names
- [ ] Browser zoom at 110 to 125 percent so text survives a small player
- [ ] Window sized to the recording frame (16:9 for most, 9:16 for social cuts)
- [ ] Microphone tested; record 5 seconds and listen back
- [ ] Light in front of you, not behind
- [ ] Water nearby, phone face down
- [ ] Script open where your eyes naturally fall (near the camera)

---

## 5. Review before you share

Watch it once with sound off and once at 1.5x speed.

- [ ] The problem is clear in the first 10 seconds
- [ ] A viewer with sound off can follow it (captions on)
- [ ] There is exactly one call to action
- [ ] No private data, tokens, customer names or internal URLs on screen
- [ ] The title says what the viewer gets, not what the product is called
- [ ] The thumbnail or first frame shows the result, not a logo

---

## 6. Variants

**Sales follow-up.** Open with the prospect's own words from the call. Show only the part of the product they asked about. End with the next meeting.

**Investor update.** Three numbers, one win, one problem, one ask. Screen only for charts; camera for the ask.

**Onboarding.** One task per video. Title it as the task ("Invite your team"). Link the next task at the end.

**Course lesson.** State the outcome of the lesson first. Record in chapters so you can update one part when the product changes.

**Internal async update.** Replace a meeting. Say the decision you need and by when.
