# Higgsfield shot brief

Free FrankX production worksheet. Guide: https://www.frankx.ai/guides/higgsfield-ai-video-guide

The filled sheet is shot 03 from that guide's studio exercise: one person, one room, one action. It is the exercise, not a recorded generation. Credit cost, model, and job id stay blank until your account shows them. Copy the blank sheet for your own shot.

## Example: shot 03, the decision

- Project and audience: First studio sequence for a creator. One person, one room, one physical action per shot. Target edit: 12 to 18 seconds. Check the selected model for the lengths it supports.
- Viewer takeaway: The person decides to begin.
- Destination and aspect ratio: Review the cut first. Export for the destination you will actually publish, in that destination's aspect ratio.
- Shot ID and intended edit length: 03. Close view, held long enough for a clean exit.
- Subject and setting: Seated person, closed notebook, piano in the same room. Same clothing, hair, and window light as shots 01 and 02.
- Authorized reference file: The approved still of this person and room. If the hand, face, or wardrobe is already wrong in the still, do not animate it.
- Character / wardrobe / product details that must stay fixed: Clothing, hair, window light, notebook position.
- Subject movement: The person takes a breath and looks toward the piano.
- One camera movement: One slow forward push, eye level. No orbit. No cut.
- Lighting and visual style: The same window light as shots 01 and 02.
- Prompt version: v1, the close-shot brief below.
- Model and settings actually selected:
- Acceptance rule (visible, checkable): The face still matches the approved still, and the expression is readable. Clothing, hair, window light, and notebook position are unchanged.
- Rejection rule: An extra person, a changed prop, a text overlay, a second camera move, or a face that no longer matches the still.
- Credit cost shown before generation:
- Maximum attempts and credit ceiling: Write both before the first render.
- Job ID:
- First / middle / last frame review: Check face, clothing, notebook, and light at each frame. The last frame needs a readable expression so the editor has an exit.
- Accepted file and edit notes: Download the accepted clip and trim it to the action. Watch the three shots with the sound off. Arrival, preparation, and decision should still read.

```text
Reference: the approved studio character and room.
Start: medium close view, seated character looking at the closed notebook.
Action: the character takes a breath and looks toward the piano.
Camera: one slow forward push, eye level, no orbit or cut.
End: hold the expression briefly so the editor has a clean exit.
Continuity: same clothing, hair, window light and notebook position.
Avoid: additional people, changing props, text overlays, scene transitions.
```

Adjust the wording to the controls your selected model actually has. Then inspect the clip. A prompt line is not proof that the picture obeyed it.
