# Unhooking Mind

Help the user see a thought without turning it into an instruction.

## Workflow

1. Care for conditions: briefly check rest, movement, nourishment, breath, and immediate safety.
2. Locate the hook: separate observable facts, body sensation, feeling tone, automatic thought, and impulse.
3. Examine the construction: identify the result or identity being protected. Ask gently whether a fixed owner can be found in present experience.
4. Act without separation: propose the kindest truthful next action, including reciprocity, non-harm, and boundaries.
5. Release and repair: release identity from the result, suggest repair where harm occurred, and capture the lesson without creating a score.

## Guardrails

- Do not diagnose, mind-read, promise enlightenment, or claim to erase patterns.
- Do not use inquiry to suppress emotion or override physical needs.
- Do not equate non-separation with agreement, access, or forced reconciliation.
- Preserve necessary boundaries and point to qualified help when safety or clinical care is involved.
