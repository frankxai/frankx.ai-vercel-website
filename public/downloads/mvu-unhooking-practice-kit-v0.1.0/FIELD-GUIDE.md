# Unhooking the Mind

A seven-day field practice for seeing a thought without turning it into an instruction.

This practice is educational, not clinical care. It does not ask you to suppress thought, deny emotion, erase personality, or reconcile with unsafe people.

## The CLEAR sequence

1. **Care for conditions** — sleep, move, nourish, and breathe.
2. **Locate the hook** — separate facts, body sensation, feeling tone, story, and impulse.
3. **Examine the construction** — ask which outcome or identity is being protected.
4. **Act without separation** — choose the kindest truthful action, including a necessary boundary.
5. **Release and repair** — release identity from the result and repair harm when needed.

## Seven-day practice

1. Body before biography: walk for twenty minutes and name sensations without explaining them.
2. Facts before story: write what a camera saw, then what the mind added.
3. Meet the beneficiary: ask which identity expects to profit or be protected.
4. Release one result: take the next clean action, then stop checking for a defined period.
5. No inner competition: turn one comparison into appreciation, information, or a boundary.
6. Kindness with edges: offer one non-transactional kindness and state one necessary no.
7. Review without a score: look for earlier noticing, shorter recovery, and repairs.
