# Weekly review

Review the week without turning practice into a ranking system.

## Conditions

- How was sleep?
- How often did I move?
- Which conditions made hooks easier or harder to see?

## Recognition

- Which hooks did I catch before acting?
- What was the median time between activation and recognition?
- Which triggers repeated?

## Relationship

- Where did comparison seed separation?
- Which non-transactional kindness felt honest?
- Which boundary or repair was necessary?

## Next experiment

Choose one small environmental or behavioural change for the coming week. Do not choose a new identity.
