## Variant 01 — clothbound (Coralie Bickford-Smith clothbound craft)

```
## CONCEPT
Premium flat ebook/print front cover for a carefully crafted book object. Craftsman material truth + Coralie Bickford-Smith clothbound craft. Pattern and object are part of the book's story.

## ART DIRECTION
School: Coralie Bickford-Smith clothbound craft. Energy: museum trust luxury.
Field pattern (L1): fine linen cloth weave, almost invisible embossed micro-motif, combined with book DNA pattern: tool grid micro.
Object mark (L2): brass drafting compass on cream paper OR locked drawer line.
FrankX nonfiction/poetry craft — no Arcanea creatures, no robots, no neural-net spam, no crypto glyphs.
FLAT graphic design 2:3 portrait — NOT a photograph of a physical hardcover on a table, NOT a 3D product mockup as the master file.

## SCENE
Full-bleed flat cover. Field color near #060b0b. Accents: #0f766e, #C9A84C, #F2E8D5.
Craftsman-quality texture and structure. One clear object or typographic hero. Quiet premium pattern in the field at low visibility (3-12%).

## COMPOSITION
2:3 (1600x2560 intent). Safe margins 8% from all edges.
Upper zone: exact title typography.
Center: object/pattern focus.
Lower: exact subtitle then author.
Title must dominate thumbnail legibility.

## LIGHTING
Museum/print craft lighting — even field, soft specular on metal/foil only. No neon bloom soup.

## PALETTE
Field #060b0b; accents #0f766e, #C9A84C, #F2E8D5; deep blacks and controlled golds. Max 3-4 colors.

## TYPOGRAPHY
EXACT STRINGS — do not invent, translate, or replace:

SUBTITLE: What Every Maker Learns Too Late
AUTHOR: Frank Riemer
Use elegant Didone or refined prestige type for literary/manifesto; strong grotesque allowed for rebel channel only (channel id clothbound).
Gold foil or high-contrast type. Never put designer names in the author line. Never put art-direction meta sentences as the subtitle.

## MOOD
museum trust luxury. Structured, patterned, inevitable — a real publishing house object.

## STYLE REFERENCES
Coralie Bickford-Smith clothbound craft; Coralie Bickford-Smith material craft; Knopf prestige; Taschen restraint; craftsman bookbinding foil work.
```
