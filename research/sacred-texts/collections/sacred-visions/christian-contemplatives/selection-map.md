# Christian Contemplatives — Selection Map

This is an editorial map, not a claim that the nine thinkers agree. The
sequence moves from inward restlessness through reason, detachment, intimacy,
darkness, existential pressure, responsibility, attention, and silence.

## First poetic constellation

The opening experience should not summarize nine systems. It should stage four
voices already cleared for original-language display:

1. Augustine — desire as restlessness.
2. Aquinas — mystery disciplined by form.
3. Teresa — surrender expressed as reciprocal belonging.
4. John — darkness transformed from absence into passage.

Eckhart, Pascal, Kierkegaard, Weil, and Merton then enter through precise
locators and original commentary until their selected editions and permissions
are resolved.

## Acceptance test for every passage

- exact work and stable location;
- source language visible;
- edition and translator visible;
- source, translation, interpretation, and new composition labeled separately;
- no quotation assembled from variants;
- no claim stronger than the source;
- no reuse mode broader than the rights evidence.
