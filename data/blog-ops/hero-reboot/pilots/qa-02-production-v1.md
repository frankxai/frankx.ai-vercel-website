# Independent QA — Pilot 02: Production Demo to Responsibility (v1)

**Verdict:** ITERATE — narrative intent is present, but the source frame is not yet a strong article-specific editorial cover.

## What worked

- It includes a real person in a consequential review moment, which is a major improvement over an abstract agent metaphor.
- No public readable text, logo, or generated dashboard is visible.
- The equipment, test wall, and human reviewer create a baseline of technical seriousness.

## Why it is not ready

1. **It reads as an electronics hardware lab, not an agentic-AI production decision.** The shelves of PCB-like equipment dominate the frame and could illustrate almost any engineering article.
2. **The central thesis is not readable at thumbnail size.** There is no clear visual contrast between "convincing demo" and "governed production"; it is simply a busy room.
3. **The composition is too wide and observational.** The viewer has no single decision point to lock onto. The person is small and passive; the wall becomes visual noise.
4. **The whiteboard marks are close to pseudo-diagram territory.** They are not legible, but they lack deterministic informational value and will compete with a title treatment.
5. **Share composition is weak.** There is no controlled area for the exact social claim without obscuring the only meaningful human action.

## Required revision

Remove the racks and generic lab inventory. Make one accountable architecture-review decision the dominant subject: one architect, one clearly structured/unstructured boundary, and one visible human checkpoint. Keep any information-bearing framework for deterministic post-production rather than model-created whiteboard noise.
