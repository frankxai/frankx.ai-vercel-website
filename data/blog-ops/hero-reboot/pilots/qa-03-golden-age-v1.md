**Verdict:** REJECT — do not compose or integrate this source frame.

## What worked

- The creator at the doorway gives the frame a human, threshold-based structure rather than a disembodied AI symbol.
- Dawn light and a modest workspace point toward real creative practice instead of luxury-founder fantasy.

## Why it fails

1. **Generated marks and probable text are visible.** Street signage, wall drawings, paper notes, and notebook content introduce model-rendered visual noise and violate the exact-type rule.
2. **The scene feels accidentally documentary rather than authored editorial.** Exposed wires, cluttered wall papers, rubbish/street detail, and the unclear handheld device weaken the premium focus.
3. **The article's structural argument is not visible.** It reads as a musician leaving a workshop, not a creator with newly compressed production and distribution capability.
4. **Social hierarchy is missing.** The doorway, person, street, and workbench all compete; a deterministic title would have no quiet, high-contrast relationship to the image.

## Required revision

Keep the modest studio-to-world threshold and human dignity. Replace the literal street and clutter with an original, controlled interior/outward composition: one creator, one unmistakable instrument/media-making trace, a quiet early-world horizon, and enough intentional negative space for the actual title. No signage, diagrams, notes, screens, or exposed-wire clutter.
