# QA — FRANK-Ω Pilot 01: Studio Co-Pilot (v1)

**Verdict:** ITERATE — emotionally strong scene, but not sufficiently identity-locked for a FrankX production character asset.

## What worked

- The human creator and a physically present robot share a credible music-making action. This is much more meaningful than a robot placed in a generic tech background.
- Warm studio craft, guitar, compact audio tools, and close human focus give the image immediate creator relevance.
- The robot reads at thumbnail scale and the violet core/cool eye treatment distinguish it from generic warm mascots.

## Why it cannot pass yet

1. **Identity drift:** the robot acquired a tall antenna/fin, overly circular white-lit eyes, and a more generic small-droid face. Official FRANK-Ω has no antenna, has large cyan-blue irised eyes, strong brow shapes, a rounded helmet silhouette, clear side earpods, and a small asymmetric metal smile.
2. **The character is too figurine-like.** It needs more mature, proportional presence beside the creator—not a desk toy that happens to have a violet light.
3. **Composition lacks a clean intentional type relationship.** The warm lamp and human face occupy the only plausible title area.
4. **Printed paper remains at the foreground edge.** It is not clearly readable, but it is unnecessary model-generated mark risk.

## Revision

Keep the real shared music-making action and warm documentary intimacy. Lock FRANK-Ω’s official helmet/eyes/earpods/brows/core exactly; remove antenna, paper, signage, and desk-toy scale. Place the duo to the right, with a deliberate dark-left editorial field for later deterministic copy.
