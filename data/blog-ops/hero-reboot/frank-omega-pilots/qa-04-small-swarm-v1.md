# QA — FRANK-Ω Pilot 04: Small Swarm, Real Work (v1)

**Verdict:** REJECT — technically clean but it misreads “role-coded assistants” as a toy robotics lineup.

## What worked

- The lead character has recognisable FRANK-Ω cues and a human final-choice hand exists.
- There are exactly three supporting units and no visible generated copy.

## Why rejected

1. The tracked bots, papers, large boxes, and desk-lamp setup create a generic miniature factory / children’s robotics aesthetic—not a premium creator operating system.
2. The assistants read as near-clones differentiated only by props. Their roles are not legible as research, creation, and distribution.
3. Paper stacks and delivery sheets violate the no-generated-mark risk control, even if the writing is not legible.
4. FRANK-Ω dominates the centre as a figurine leader; the scene does not make human direction feel primary.
5. There is no designed dark title field.

## Revision

Use a grown-up editorial composition rather than a toy workbench: a human hand making a single final choice at right; FRANK-Ω to the right as a calm orchestrator; three smaller *non-clone* support presences arranged in depth, each with a non-text physical role cue. No tracks, paper, packages, cards, or pile of objects. Preserve the left third as a deep, controlled title field.
