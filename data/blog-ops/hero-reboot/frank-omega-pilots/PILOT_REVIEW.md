## System conclusion

FRANK-Ω materially improves recognisability and approachability when he **plays a necessary editorial role**. The strongest examples preserve human agency and use him as a companion, reviewer, witness, or bounded orchestrator. The character must not become a universal header substitute.

The shared deep-left title area is a composition choice for these pilots, not a new global template. Exact public title copy is still composed deterministically per surface; no image-model text is approved.
