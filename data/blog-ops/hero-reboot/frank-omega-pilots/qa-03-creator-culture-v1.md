# QA — FRANK-Ω Pilot 03: Creator-Culture Cover (v1)

**Verdict:** ITERATE — emotionally promising, but fails the no-fake-UI rule and is too close to the Studio Co-Pilot visual territory.

## What worked

- Human creative focus, dawn horizon, guitar, microphone, and an observant FRANK-Ω create real cultural feeling rather than a generic “future of AI” image.
- FRANK-Ω remains recognisable through the gunmetal head, cyan eyes, violet core, and compact body.

## Blocking issues

1. **Fake UI:** the laptop screen visibly contains generated coloured software panels. This cannot represent evidence and cannot ship.
2. **Generated note/paper marks:** small paper artifacts add unnecessary text risk.
3. **Territory overlap:** it is another desk-and-audio-workstation scene. The Golden Age essay needs a wider cultural composition and a more deliberate editorial distinction from the ACOS studio documentary.
4. **Title area is weak:** every region is visually active; it needs a more intentional copy relationship.

## Revision

Remove laptop, any screen, papers, and in-frame generated marks. Keep the dawn window, but make it central to the wider-possible-world thesis. Use a magazine-cover composition: a creator in the foreground, FRANK-Ω at the window edge as a quiet witness, guitar/microphone as sparse physical signals, and a darker left field for deterministic title overlay.
