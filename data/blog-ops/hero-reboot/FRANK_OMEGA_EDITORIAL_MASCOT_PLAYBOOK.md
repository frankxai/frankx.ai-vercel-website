# FRANK-Ω Editorial Mascot Playbook

**Status:** Controlled pilot contract — 2026-07-17
**Scope:** FrankX editorial headers and social cards only. This does not authorize a site-wide mascot takeover or a GenCreator identity merge.

## Why use FRANK-Ω

FRANK-Ω can make difficult intelligence, architecture, and creator-system subjects more approachable **when he plays an article-specific role**: guide, reviewer, co-creator, or responsible orchestrator. He is not decorative wallpaper, a replacement for FrankX’s wordmark, or a universal hero solution.

## Official visual truth

Use the approved primary reference:

`public/images/mascot/frank-omega-pixar-blue-v1.png`

### Character DNA — lock

- **Species:** full Pixar-chibi robot droid; never human, cyborg-human, animal, or anime character.
- **Silhouette:** oversized rounded helmet head; compact articulated body; earpod-like side forms; strong chibi proportions; planted, capable stance.
- **Face:** very large luminous cyan-blue eyes, expressive dark brows, small asymmetric confident metal smile; direct and warm, never vacant or angry.
- **Materials:** gunmetal / graphite segmented shell, subtly polished but tactile—not chrome luxury-product styling.
- **Signature:** violet circular chest core; cyan eyes; restrained violet rim light. These three cues must survive thumbnail scale.
- **Personality:** direct, competent, observant, warm underneath, a little MGK cool; “already knows the answer,” never hyperactive mascot cheerleading.

### Hard exclusions

- no human skin, hair, clothing, or human face;
- no green GEN-Ω core, constellation crown, GenCreator mark, or GenCreator palette takeover;
- no generic robot, robot army, floating head, toy figurine product shot, superhero pose, or mascot as a logo;
- no generated public words, code, UI, dashboards, labels, signs, or logos;
- no more than **three** supporting bots, and only for an article explicitly about coordinated roles;
- no mascot on a serious article unless it clarifies the reader’s relationship to the system.

## Usage decision

| Article type | FRANK-Ω role | Use? |
|---|---|---|
| Creator workflow / ACOS / music practice | Quiet operating companion | Strong fit |
| Architecture / governance / model decisions | Accountable reviewer or gatekeeper | Strong fit if grounded in the actual decision |
| Culture / creator-economy essay | Editorial witness or co-creator | Use sparingly |
| Swarm / multi-agent coordination | Lead with a small role-coded support crew | Strong fit, max three supports |
| Research, legal, news, sensitive analysis | Usually no mascot | Prefer evidence-led visual system |

## Four controlled pilot modes

1. **Studio Co-Pilot** — warm documentary scene; FRANK-Ω is a believable partner in a human creator’s real practice.
2. **Architecture Gatekeeper** — technically credible material decision; FRANK-Ω makes responsibility visible without fake UI.
3. **Creator-Culture Cover** — culturally resonant editorial composition; FRANK-Ω supports the creator’s possibility rather than replacing them.
4. **Small Swarm, Real Work** — a max-three support crew only where coordinated roles are the article’s actual point; never a toy parade.

## Production requirements

1. Start from the official primary reference image in every character generation request.
2. Create the base scene with native Grok CLI only; exact type is composed separately and deterministically.
3. Generate one source frame at a time; QA it before the next direction.
4. Test that FRANK-Ω’s violet core, cyan eyes, and head silhouette remain identifiable at 320px.
5. A mascot pilot must pass both article-fidelity and character-integrity review. A cute robot image is not sufficient.
6. Treat current `1280×720` renders as pilots, not final premium masters.
