# Independent Red-Team Verdict

**Source:** delegated external creative-director review, 2026-07-17
**Verdict:** retire all four FRANK-Ω pilots from flagship-hero consideration.

## What the red team confirmed

The early pilots were technically polished but failed the editorial job:

- mascot-first ideation replaced article thesis with “where can the robot stand?”;
- robot + dark studio + warm lamp + cyan eyes is already a campaign recipe, not a differentiated hero language;
- title-safe voids carried compositions that could not stand on their own;
- cinema was used as atmosphere rather than consequence;
- concrete article proof—commands, skills, workflows, four maturity tiers, six planes, state contracts, 12,000 tracks, $90/month infrastructure—was not visualised;
- the mascot stole attention from the human maker or decision-maker without performing an indispensable role.

## Replacement territories

| Territory | Use | Required production route | FRANK-Ω rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working Session | creator systems and practice | real working evidence + deterministic editorial annotation | only when an actual assistant relationship is the article subject; ≤15% of frame, never front-facing or center-stage |
| Decision Instrument | architecture, governance, technical guides | deterministic SVG/HTML/Figma-style composition using exact article data | absent |
| Thesis Cover | creator culture and essays | verified documentary/commissioned source or real Frank-owned artifacts; title-led cover | absent |

## Binding kill criteria

Reject a concept before polishing if it fails the swap, mascot, toy, void, evidence, human-truth, thumbnail, mood, title-rescue, article-fidelity, reference-imitation, or cuteness-override test. In particular: a synthetic stranger must not impersonate a first-person FrankX argument, and a technical guide must not substitute AI-shaped objects for its actual decision model.

## Council implementation decision

The red-team result changes the next production move:

1. The current xAI source-frame tests remain documented **rejections/iteration evidence**, not candidates to improve with another prompt.
2. The production-architecture article advances through a deterministic **Decision Instrument** concept board with exact article terms and an explicit Tier-2 boundary.
3. The ACOS article advances only when real working-session evidence can be sourced; no generated desk still-life substitute.
4. The Golden Age thesis advances only with a verified documentary/commissioned Frank-owned source, not an AI-generated anonymous creator.
