# Candidate Render Card — The Compounding Sheet

**Status:** council candidate · not yet approved to render
**Article:** *The Complete Guide to Agentic Creator OS v6: Your AI Operating System*
**Surface archetype:** editorial cover / studio artifact

## Image thesis

> Fragmented creative tools become valuable only when they are arranged as one authored operating system.

The reader should recognise the feeling of finally having an ordered creative practice—not “a robot helps a person make things.”

## Composition

- A near-black 16:9 field carries one oversized archival working sheet, almost top-down, cropped decisively by the frame.
- The sheet contains a **single ordered relay** of five physically distinct, wordless inserts: graphite strip, translucent cyan acetate, narrow amber timing ruler, blackened-metal binder spine, violet registration marker.
- One cropped adult hand is caught placing the final blank card. No face, no scene, no lifestyle performance.
- The system relay begins inside the type zone. Deterministic title is later set **on the sheet**, not parked in empty background.
- The image must remain recognisable at 320px as *a deliberate working artifact becoming a system*.

## Material and light law

- Warm off-white paper fibre, ink/graphite, semi-matte acetate, anodised metal, honest surface wear.
- One raking studio key from upper right; near-black surround; no ambient neon, haze, or decorative glow.
- Cyan appears only as a thin acetate/registration signal. Violet only as a small registration mark. Amber only as the timing ruler.
- The result should feel like an authored artifact photographed for a contemporary technical-cultural essay, not product photography, a dashboard, or a moodboard.

## Strict exclusions

- FRANK-Ω character figure, robot, face, or mascot scene.
- Laptop, monitor, UI, code, generated type, logo, glyph, letter, or watermark.
- Human-and-robot collaboration cliché.
- Floating hologram, desk lamp hero, guitar shorthand, studio-stock pose, cinematic bokeh, cyan glow recipe, left-side safe zone.
