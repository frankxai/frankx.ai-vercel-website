# FrankX Hero Design Council — Reboot

**Status:** active creative reset · draft-only · no production integration
**Reason:** The first FRANK-Ω pilots were technically competent but creatively weak. They treated a mascot as a scene prop, repeated familiar AI-cinema staging, and mistook prompt specificity for art direction.

## Decision to make

Choose **one original, article-specific visual territory** worth turning into a 2K+ source master and a composed 16:9 / OG / 4:5 / 9:16 asset family. Do not generate a second batch until the council kills the weak directions and selects a winner.

## Hard diagnosis of the rejected pilots

1. **Scene-first AI slop:** all four use an expected generated-image grammar: moody workstation, glowing objects, a friendly robot, and an implied task. That is not a FrankX point of view.
2. **The wrong scale:** FRANK-Ω became either a desk toy or an illustrative sidekick. The official character is a confident digital twin with graphic presence and clean silhouette.
3. **No designed image/type relationship:** every image relies on a left blank-ish field. That is a layout habit, not editorial composition.
4. **The content promise is invisible:** a reader cannot tell why *this* image belongs to ACOS instead of any generic AI-creator post.
5. **Generative realism without cultural truth:** the human scenes have no specific authored ritual, real evidence, or lived consequence; they land as stock-adjacent.

## Design laws for the next round

- Start with a **single sentence that the image proves without a title**.
- Choose one surface archetype per asset: **editorial cover**, **technical proof plate**, or **studio artifact**. Never mix all three.
- Build image and deterministic type as **one composition**, not image plus a safe zone.
- Use FRANK-Ω only as a distinct editorial instrument: a portrait, an annotation, a silhouette, or a witness. No human-and-robot-at-a-desk staging.
- Prefer real proof, a designed system, an authored set, or a graphic motif over an invented lifestyle scene.
- Do not copy a living artist, publication, or brand. References are principles: editorial hierarchy, material restraint, cinematic crop discipline, and exact typography.
- A concept fails if it could appear on a generic AI newsletter, uses a floating hologram, pseudo-interface, sci-fi control room, vague neon, or a robot holding/pointing at something.

## Gate sequence

1. Five council seats independently write a verdict.
2. Chair synthesizes exactly **three** distinct territories.
3. Each territory gets one concept card and a 30-second thumbnail test.
4. Red-team kills at least two weak options or identifies why none deserve rendering.
5. Only the winner gets one high-resolution source-master render; no bulk batch.
6. The winner then gets deterministic text and context QA before any site integration.

## Approval boundary

This council may create briefs, prompts, review evidence, and draft assets only. It cannot modify article frontmatter, production mappings, deploys, or external posts.
