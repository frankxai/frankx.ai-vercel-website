# QA — 01 The Compounding Sheet (ACOS)

**Verdict:** **REJECT** as a final art direction. Retain only as council iteration evidence.

## What worked

- The human-and-robot-at-a-desk grammar is gone.
- Camera angle, restrained palette, tactile paper/metal, and a single cropped hand create more authored material presence than the rejected mascot scenes.
- The cyan / amber / violet restriction is controlled rather than a neon recipe.

## Why it still fails

1. **The article claim does not survive the image.** It reads as an architect/designer's drafting table, not as creative tools becoming a compounding operating system.
2. **It is still a prop arrangement.** The cyan strip, ruler, rail, dot, and card are five handsome objects, not a visible system or transformation.
3. **Generated marks break the source-frame contract.** The graphite strip has pseudo-handwriting; the ruler carries markings; the source therefore cannot be trusted as a clean premium master.
4. **The sheet is a large field, not a designed image/type relationship.** It risks becoming exactly the empty field the council set out to stop.
5. **Thumbnail story is too weak.** At feed scale it will read as stationery / drafting tools, not an Agentic Creator OS.
