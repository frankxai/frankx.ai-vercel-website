# Council Brief — What FrankX Needs Instead

## Reader reality

FrankX is for creators and operators who are tired of generic AI optimism. They want evidence that one person can build serious creative and technical systems without losing taste, judgment, or a human point of view.

## The editorial opportunity

The hero must make the reader feel: **"This is not an AI-content machine. This is an authored studio with an operating system behind it."**

## Three article truths to protect

| Article | Image-worthy proof | Emotional posture | Wrong visual shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| *The Complete Guide to Agentic Creator OS* | Fragmented tools become a personal creative operating system that compounds. | relief → agency | generic robot helper / dashboard collage |
| *Enterprise Agentic Architecture: Decision Framework for Production* | A good production system chooses constraints before it automates. | seriousness → clarity | glowing server room / dashboard fantasy |
| *The Golden Age of Creators* | A solo creator can make studio-scale work because systems compound, not because they hustle harder. | possibility → resolve | aspirational person-at-laptop or AI utopia |

## Official FRANK-Ω posture to preserve

The official page establishes an unusually clean relationship: near-black field, severe typography, crisp cyan-to-violet signal, a self-contained portrait, and a calm figure whose confidence comes from restraint. He is a **digital twin**, not a Pixar scene accessory.

## Output required from every council seat

1. Name the exact failure in the old four-pilot family.
2. Propose three territories that are genuinely different at the level of composition and story—not colour.
3. Give a one-line image thesis and one concept card per territory.
4. State how title typography coexists with image from the first sketch.
5. State who/what must be absent.
6. Give a binary render recommendation: `RENDER`, `HOLD`, or `KILL`.

## Constraints

- Native Grok/xAI only for source-frame generation.
- Exact public copy is composed deterministically, never generated.
- All current renders are rejected as final art direction; do not defend them.
- FRANK-Ω is optional. A stronger concept without him wins over a weaker concept with him.
- Target a high-resolution source master; 1280×720 is pilot-only.
- No production integration without Frank's approval.
