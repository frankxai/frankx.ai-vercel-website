# QA — 03 Threshold Capacity (Golden Age)

**Verdict:** **REJECT** as a final art direction. Retain only as council evidence.

## What worked

- It avoids mascot-sidekick grammar and overt AI imagery.
- The ordinary courtyard, cool blue hour, and small human figure have more human truth than an aspirational luxury loft.
- The diagonal production rail creates a readable structural device.

## Why it fails

1. The large charcoal wall is still a title-safe void, rather than a designed collision of image and type.
2. The person hanging a bare bulb reads as an art-school / lifestyle-film gesture, not an image of compounding system capacity.
3. The rail artifacts feel staged and generic; they do not make studio-scale output visible.
4. The image has mood but lacks a concrete visual claim. Without a title, a viewer sees an empty room and a person arranging props.
5. A small fake label appears on the rail, so the source is not clean enough to be a premium master.

## Decision

Do not rescue this by adding title over the empty wall. That would turn the composition failure into a template. The direction needs a materially stronger evidence system before it earns another render.
