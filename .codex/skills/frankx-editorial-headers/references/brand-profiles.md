# Brand profiles

## FrankX

- Job: evidence-led systems editorial for founders and AI architects.
- Feeling: late-night studio, technical authority, quiet confidence.
- Palette: obsidian `#0A0A0B`, emerald `#10B981`, cyan `#06B6D4`, warm white.
- Type: Poppins display, Inter body/UI, JetBrains Mono technical labels, sparse Playfair accent.
- Signature: cinematic system artifact plus exact live headline, one emerald structural line, evidence or capability rail.
- Identity: FrankX mark or `FrankX.AI` lockup is primary. External marks remain smaller and relationship-neutral.
- Refuse: generic neon AI brains, card wallpaper, gratuitous logo rows, decorative gradients, title-free flagship art.

## Starlight

- Job: sovereign intelligence infrastructure and high-consequence system design.
- Feeling: scientific, enduring, governed, premium.
- Palette: deep obsidian, controlled cobalt/cyan, sparse stellar gold.
- Type: exact Starlight identity profile first; technical sans and mono dominate.
- Signature: control-plane topology, evidence ledger, boundary or constellation logic.
- Refuse: sci-fi spectacle without an architectural claim.

## Arcanea

- Job: mythic learning, worldbuilding, and creative academy experiences.
- Feeling: luminous, authored, cinematic, invitational.
- Palette: approved Arcanea violet, celestial cyan, gold, and deep night.
- Type: exact Arcanea display and body profiles; serif may lead when the brand constitution permits.
- Signature: one character, place, relic, or academy symbol with narrative meaning; exact crest on a quiet field.
- Refuse: importing Arcanea mythology into FrankX, generic fantasy collage, or generated crest geometry.

When a brand profile is missing or conflicts with a current brand constitution, stop and resolve the identity source before final export.
